Type 40 questions

What operators ask us first

Do you have a San Francisco Type 40 for sale?

Not today. There is no Type 40 on our San Francisco book. What we run on this class is the application, and if one comes to us for resale it goes on the book priced, like every other license here.

Do I have to serve food on a Type 40?

You do not need a full restaurant, but the ABC expects food to be available. Sandwiches, pizza or packaged snacks are the register of it, and we will tell you what your district expects to see.

Can I serve wine or cocktails too?

No. Wine and distilled spirits are not permitted on this class. A beer and wine room is a Type 41; a full bar is a Type 47 or a Type 48, depending on whether the kitchen carries the business.

Are minors allowed in?

Generally yes, provided they do not buy or drink alcohol and your local rules and your own house policy allow it.

Can I put a band on?

The license governs alcohol service, not entertainment. Live music, noise and occupancy run on local permits and conditions, and we coordinate the license with them rather than leaving you to find out they were separate.

How long does a Type 40 take?

Typically several months from filing to issuance, depending on district workload, background checks, and any local hearing or protest. A transfer runs on a different clock: a median of 78 days across our 64 closed California files.

Should I apply, or buy a license instead?

Tell us what you are opening and we will tell you. If beer is the whole point of the room, applying is usually the conversation; if you need wine or spirits as well, one of the classes with stock is, and we handle either.
