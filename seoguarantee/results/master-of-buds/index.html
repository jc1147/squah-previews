The story

How Master of Buds got there

  1. 01

    Who they are

    Master of Buds is one of the first cannabis dispensaries in the Tulsa, Oklahoma area. It carries a wide assortment of cannabis products: buds, edibles and tonics, pre-rolls and accessories, vape pens and concentrates.

    It also offers doctor recommendation services, so a new patient can start visiting its medical dispensary for medical marijuana products, with a doctor's consultation in person or over Zoom.

    Master of Buds is one of our Full Suite clients: one team delivered their website, their SEO and the automation behind them.
