How patients choose

What a Las Vegas client checks before booking a treatment

Your next client usually searches for a treatment, not a spa: lip filler before a wedding, laser hair removal before summer, a facial the week of an event. Then they look at photos, read reviews and check who does the work before they book. Many want a consultation before the first treatment.

They leave at small snags: before-and-after photos that look like stock images, no word on who injects, a booking page that fails on a phone, a review nobody answered. Questions matter too: a client comparing two spas wants to know what a visit involves, how long it takes and what aftercare looks like before committing.

We fix those before we build anything new: your own consented photos, labeled with the treatment and a note that results vary, and each provider named with their license. Answer Step 1 and say which treatments you most want booked.

The channels

Med spa SEO and marketing in Las Vegas: the five channels we run

Digital marketing for a med spa rests on five channels, and we work them as one, because a strong treatment page with a thin profile, or great reviews with no photos, still loses the booking.

  1. 01

    Local SEO for med spas

    Clients search for a med spa close to home or work, and the map favors spas near the person searching. We give each location its own page, name the neighborhoods you draw from and keep your spa's name, address and phone matching everywhere you are listed.

  2. 02

    Google Business Profile for med spas

    Your profile is often the first thing a client sees. We list every treatment, choose the right categories, post real photos of your rooms and team, keep hours and booking links exact and answer the questions people leave. Many clients book straight from it, so its booking link has to work on a phone.

  3. 03

    A page for every treatment

    Clients type fillers, wrinkle relaxers, laser hair removal and IV drips straight into the search bar. Each treatment gets its own page saying what it involves, who performs it and what recovery looks like, and none promises a result.

  4. 04

    A med spa website that books the appointment

    Clients want to book the moment they decide. We put online booking on every treatment page, make pages quick on a phone and track which pages bring in bookings; a full redesign sits in our Full Suite.

  5. 05

    Reviews and real results for Las Vegas med spas

    Reviews and real results weigh heavily with clients comparing spas. We help you ask every client for a review, show before-and-after photos only from consenting clients with a note that results vary, and reply to reviews without confirming anyone's treatment, even when the review names it.

Your practice type

SEO and marketing for each kind of med spa in Las Vegas

Your patients search for a treatment, not for a med spa in general, so every service you offer is found through its own searches. Answer Step 1 and name the treatments you want more of; we start there.

  • Injectables and filler marketing

    People search for wrinkle relaxers, lip filler or cheek filler, then compare before-and-after photos and the credentials of whoever holds the needle. We build a page for each treatment with your own photos, shown with consent and labeled with what was done, naming each injector's license and any supervising physician.

  • Nurse injector marketing

    Nurse injectors with their own following are searched by name and by treatment, and patients want to see the license, the training and the medical oversight behind them. Nurse injector marketing should show exactly that: we list your license, the physician or prescriber you work with, and only the treatments your state lets you give.

  • Laser hair removal marketing

    People search for laser hair removal by body area and price per session, and they book the clinic that explains the course, the prep and who operates the laser. Laser hair removal marketing done right says hair reduction, never hair gone forever, and names the person on your team who runs each session.

  • Body contouring marketing

    People search for non-surgical fat reduction, body sculpting or skin tightening and compare devices, session counts and real results before booking. Body contouring marketing has to stay honest: we give each device you own its own page, show your results with consent, say results vary and may not last, and never sell it as weight loss.

  • Microneedling and skin rejuvenation marketing

    Skin rejuvenation patients compare microneedling, peels and lasers, often by photos of skin tone and texture like their own. We write a page for each option you offer that says what it treats in the terms its device is authorized for, shows your own results with consent, and names the licensed provider who performs it.

  • Facials and chemical peel marketing

    People search for hydrating facials, chemical peels and dermaplaning near them, often the week before an event, and book whoever shows open times and real reviews. We write a page for each facial and peel you offer, with what it involves, how long it takes and who performs it.

  • IV therapy marketing

    People search for IV hydration, vitamin drips or a hangover IV, often wanting one the same day, and they pick whoever explains what is in the bag and who gives it. Our IV therapy marketing lists each drip's ingredients, names the clinician who assesses and supervises, and keeps every health claim to what studies support.

  • Medical weight loss marketing

    Weight loss patients want a program run by someone licensed to prescribe, with clear visit schedules and honest expectations, and they leave sites that read like ads. We write your program pages to name the prescriber, say plainly whether each medicine is approved or compounded, and show results only with a note on what patients generally achieve.

  • Hormone replacement therapy marketing

    Men and women looking into hormone care often read for weeks, and they choose the clinic that explains lab tests, risks and follow-up in plain words. Our pages do that for each therapy you offer, name who prescribes, and follow the FDA's position that compounded hormones are not approved and not shown to be safer.

  • Full-service med spa marketing

    Many patients cannot tell which med spa treatments are medical or who is responsible for their care, so a site that makes it clear earns the booking. Each treatment you offer gets its own page saying who performs it and who supervises it, and your Business Profile lists every service accurately.

Beyond SEO, what goes into med spa marketing in Las Vegas, and what does the guarantee promise?

Our first page guarantee covers where your spa ranks on Google, never a promise of bookings or new clients. Med spa marketing reaches past search to your Business Profile, reviews, photos, referrals and a site that books, and we do that work around the rankings; if your site needs a rebuild, that is part of our Full Suite.

Do you do med spa SEO for single-treatment clinics as well as full-service med spas in Las Vegas?

Yes. Each treatment you offer, from wrinkle relaxers and fillers to IV drips and hormone care, gets pages built for the patients who search for it. Because Nevada limits who may inject fillers and wrinkle relaxers, every page names the provider, their license and any supervising physician, so what patients read matches who treats them.

Can I show before-and-after photos on my Las Vegas med spa's website?

Yes, your own. We use photos of consenting clients only, label each with the treatment, and add a note that results vary, since a result shown in an ad reads as typical unless you say otherwise. Stock or borrowed photos never appear on your pages.

Will you market my spa to clients across the valley, not only in the city?

Yes. Many clients live in Henderson, North Las Vegas or the communities between them, and they search with those names. We plan pages for the areas you actually draw from and your Business Profile lists those same areas.

Can you advertise medical weight loss and IV therapy honestly?

Yes. We describe each program as it is: who prescribes, whether a medicine is FDA-approved or compounded, and what a visit involves. We leave out promises about weight, energy or immunity that no one can stand behind.
