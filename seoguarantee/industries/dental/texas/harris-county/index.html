Choosing a dentist here

How Harris County patients settle on a dentist

In a county this spread out, most patients want a dentist near home or near work, and they search from wherever they are standing. They look for their treatment, a plan you accept and an opening that fits a long commute, then read the latest reviews before they call or book online.

Language and new arrivals shape the rest. Many households speak Spanish at home, and families moving in from other counties and states have no dentist yet. We make sure your pages say which languages your team speaks, whether you take new patients and which plans you accept, the three things these patients check first.

Then we fix what turns them away: hours that do not match, a booking link that stalls on mobile, a service on your menu that no page mentions. Begin Step 1 and say which of those patients you would like more of, and which you already see plenty of.

My office is in a suburb outside Houston. Can you still help?

Yes. A large share of the county's dentists practice outside any city limit, and their patients search by community name. We build your pages around the communities you actually draw from, whether that is Cypress, Spring, Atascocita or Pasadena.

Which parts of marketing a Harris County dental office needs do you handle, and what is your first page guarantee?

Our side of it covers search, your Business Profile, the requests for reviews and a page for every treatment you want booked; website design and conversion belong to our Full Suite. The first page guarantee concerns your practice’s rankings on Google and never promises new patients.

We run three offices across the county. Does each need its own page?

Yes. Google shows each location on the map for its own neighborhood, so each one gets a page, a profile and reviews of its own. Offices on opposite sides of the county draw different neighborhoods, and their pages should say which ones.

Can you write our pages in Spanish?

Yes, for every treatment your Spanish-speaking team provides. The Spanish pages follow the same Texas wording rules as your English pages, and a Spanish speaker on your staff approves each one before it is published.

Can we put patient testimonials on our website?

We keep testimonials out of the pages we write for you, because Texas law lists an ad that contains one among misleading ads. Photos of your own patients are a different matter: with written consent and the line that actual results may vary, the board allows them.

We practice near the county line. Do you plan for patients from the next county?

Yes. Houston and several suburbs spill over into Fort Bend and Montgomery counties, and patients cross the line without thinking about it. If yours come from places such as Katy, Sugar Land or The Woodlands, we give those places pages too, as long as your office can really serve them.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, 2025 annual averages.

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved into Harris County in a typical year † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another Texas county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Individual dentists with a practice address in Harris County, by the city or census designated place (TIGER/Line 2025) the address falls in † is derived: the same Harris County dentists, counted by the city or unincorporated community each practice address falls in, using the 2025 census boundaries.

  • Dentists with a practice address in Harris County (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry whose practice address places inside Harris County; a floor that also counts dental school faculty and residents, not only dentists in private practice.

  • Dentists outside every city in the county † is derived: the county's dentist records whose practice address falls outside every incorporated city, with military installations left out.

  • Dentists in Harris County by main field † is derived: the county's dentists counted by the main field each lists (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Average receipts per dental office † is derived: the county's 2022 dental office receipts divided by the number of those offices.

  • Residents per dental office † is derived: the county's July 2022 population estimate divided by the 2022 count of dental offices in the county.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.
