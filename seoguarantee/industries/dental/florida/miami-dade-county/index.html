Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, 2025 annual averages.

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved into Miami-Dade County in a typical year † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another Florida county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Dentists with a practice address in Miami-Dade County (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry whose practice address places inside Miami-Dade County; a floor that also counts faculty and residents.

  • Dentists outside the cities and towns † is derived: the county's dentist records whose practice address falls outside every incorporated city, town and village, with military bases left out.

  • Dentists in Miami-Dade County by main field † is derived: the county's dentists counted by the main field each lists (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Average receipts per dental office † is derived: Miami-Dade County dental offices' 2022 receipts divided by the number of those offices.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.

Rules cited on this page

  • ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct (2026): section 5.I (version dated 2026-02-18).

  • Florida Administrative Code, rule chapter 64B5-4 (Board of Dentistry: Advertising): FAC 64B5-4.002.

  • Florida Statutes, chapter 466 (Dentistry, Dental Hygiene, and Dental Laboratories): FS 466.019, FS 466.0282.
