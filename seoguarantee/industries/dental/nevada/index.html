The rules we write inside

Nevada's dental advertising rules, and how we follow them

Protecting your license comes first, so every page we write for your practice follows the state's dental advertising rules. Nevada's dental law treats false or misleading advertising, and claiming to be better than neighboring dentists, as unprofessional conduct. The dental board's rules go further: no promise about how a treatment will feel, and no extravagant or self-praising claims.

The board licenses only the twelve specialties listed below. An ad may present a dentist as a specialist in one of them only with that specialty license and its number; a dentist without it may still say the practice is limited to one area, if the ad also says the dentist is not licensed as a specialist there. A general dentist may announce any service, as long as the page says a general dentist provides it, and the profession's ethics code holds your website and its SEO to the same truthfulness. Can a general dentist in Nevada advertise braces or implants?

Yes, you can say your practice offers them. What the board restricts is wording that presents you as a specialist without the matching license, so we describe the service beside general dentistry, or, if your practice is limited to one area, we say so in the words the rule allows.

What is included in dental marketing for a Nevada practice, and where does the first page guarantee fit?

Dental marketing takes in search, your Business Profile, reviews, referrals and a website that books. Our first page guarantee sits on the search side: it is about where your practice ranks in Nevada, never a promise of new patients. The rest we build around it, so a search turns into a booked visit.

Do you cover practices outside Las Vegas and Reno?

Yes. We plan your pages around the towns your patients come from, wherever your offices are. Carson City, Elko and the smaller towns have patients searching too, often with fewer practices answering them well.

Will you write pages for my specialty?

Yes, around the procedures you perform and the words your patients use, with your license number wherever a page presents you as a specialist. For an area the board does not license, we describe the service without specialist wording.

Do Nevada's advertising rules affect my reviews and photos?

They affect what you claim. We keep patient photos consented, never promise how a treatment will feel and avoid saying you are better than other dentists. Reviews are the patient's own words; your replies stay factual and protect their privacy.

Can you help a practice with offices in Reno and the Las Vegas valley?

Yes. The two regions search differently and compete with different practices, so each office gets its own pages and profile, while your practice name and standards stay the same across both.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 25, 2026.

  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, 2025 annual averages.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • dentists with a practice address in the county † is derived: the Nevada dentist records whose practice address places inside Clark County; a floor.

  • People who moved to Nevada in a typical year † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Dentists by county † is derived: the same Nevada dentist records, counted by the county each practice address falls in.

  • Dentists with a practice address in Nevada (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry whose practice address places inside Nevada; a floor that also counts faculty and residents, not only dentists in private practice.

  • Dentists in Nevada by main field † is derived: the Nevada dentists counted by the main field each lists (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Average receipts per dental office † is derived: Nevada dental offices' 2022 receipts divided by the number of Nevada dental offices.

  • Residents per dental office † is derived: Nevada's July 2022 population estimate divided by the 2022 count of Nevada dental offices.
