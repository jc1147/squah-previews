How Las Vegas patients choose a dentist, and where you lose them

Your next patient may not search for just “a dentist”. Many search for what they need: a Saturday appointment, a same-day visit for a cracked tooth, braces for a teenager, a dentist who takes their insurance. Your license comes first

Dental marketing written inside Nevada's rules

Your license matters more than any ranking, so we write every page inside Nevada's dental advertising rules. Nevada's dental law treats claiming to be better than neighboring dentists as unprofessional conduct, so we never claim you are better than the dentist down the street. The dental board's rules let an ad present a dentist as a specialist only with that specialty license and its number, so if you hold a specialty license, every page we write for that specialty shows its number. Begin with Step 1 and we will review your site’s wording. The full rules are on Dental SEO in Nevada.

What is local SEO for dentists?

Local SEO is the part of dental SEO that works on the map results and the searches with a place in them, such as a dentist in Las Vegas or a dentist near me. It rests on your Google Business Profile, your reviews, a page for each office and the same practice details on every site that lists you. Those details are how Google tells your office from the others nearby, so we fix them first.

What does dental marketing include for a Las Vegas practice, and what does your first page guarantee cover?

Dental marketing is everything that brings a patient to your chair: search, your Business Profile, reviews, referrals and your website. When we say guaranteed dental marketing, we mean a first page guarantee: it is about where your practice ranks, never a promise of new patients. Around it we do the work that turns a search into a booking: your profile, your reviews, a page for each treatment and, in our Full Suite, your website's design and conversion work.

Do you work with dental practices like mine?

Yes. Three med spas and two dental practices we have worked for show up on page one of Google when patients in their cities search. After an assessment, we tell you which of your treatments we can take on first.

Do you do SEO for orthodontists, oral surgeons and other dental specialists in Las Vegas?

Yes. Orthodontists, endodontists, periodontists, pediatric dentists, oral surgeons and prosthodontists each get pages built around their own treatments, in the words their patients search with. If referrals bring you patients, we also build a page for the dentists who send them.

Can a general dentist advertise braces or implants in Nevada?

Yes. You can say your practice offers braces or implants. What Nevada's dental board rules restrict is wording that suggests you are a specialist when you do not hold that specialty license. So we write "we offer braces" beside "general dentistry", never "braces specialist", unless your license says so.

Do you work with dental practices outside the Las Vegas city limits?

Yes. We plan your pages around where your patients live, in the city or anywhere else in the valley.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 24, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • Net migration into Clark County † is derived: the Census Bureau's yearly net migration estimates for Clark County (moved in minus moved out), added up from April 2020 to July 2025.

  • Dentists with a practice address in the city (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry with a practice address in the Las Vegas ZIP codes was placed on the map; the count keeps those inside the 2025 city boundary, so it is a floor, and it includes university faculty and residents, not only dentists in private practice.

  • Dentists with a Las Vegas address outside the city † is derived: the same records whose practice address says Las Vegas but falls outside the city boundary, counted by the Nevada city or census place each falls in.

  • Dentists in the city by main field † is derived: the dentists inside the city limits, counted by the main field each lists in NPPES (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.

Rules cited on this page

  • ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct (2026): section 5.I (version dated 2026-02-18).

  • Nevada Administrative Code, chapter 631 (Board of Dental Examiners of Nevada): NAC 631.270 (version dated 2026-06-07).

  • Nevada Revised Statutes, chapter 631 (Dentistry and Dental Hygiene): NRS 631.348 (version dated 2026-04-21).
