How patients choose

What a Phoenix patient weighs before choosing a dentist

Your next patient probably starts with a specific need: a crown that cracked on a Friday, braces for a middle schooler, a cleaning before a plan runs out at year's end. They compare reviews, hours and photos, check the map for distance and book with the office that answers most clearly.

Heat and distance shape the choice in Phoenix. Patients avoid long midday drives in summer, favor offices close to home or work and often want early or evening appointments, so pages that say which neighborhoods you serve and when you are open do real work.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved to Phoenix in a typical year (at least) † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; moves from elsewhere in Maricopa County are not in the survey table, so it is a floor; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Dentists with a practice address in the city (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry with a practice address in the Phoenix ZIP codes was placed on the map; the count keeps those inside the 2025 city boundary, so it is a floor, and it includes faculty and residents, not only dentists in private practice.

  • Dentists in the city by main field † is derived: the dentists inside the city limits, counted by the main field each lists in NPPES (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Average receipts per dental office † is derived: the city's dental offices' 2022 receipts divided by the number of dental offices in the city.

  • Residents per dental office † is derived: the city's July 2022 population estimate divided by the 2022 count of dental offices in the city.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.

Rules cited on this page

  • Arizona Administrative Code, title 4, chapter 11 (State Board of Dental Examiners): A.A.C. R4-11-1101, A.A.C. R4-11-1102 (version dated 2025-10-19).

  • Arizona Revised Statutes, section 32-1201.01 (dentistry: definition of unprofessional conduct): ARS 32-1201.01 (version dated 2026-09-09).
