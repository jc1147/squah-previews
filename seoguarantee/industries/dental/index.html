How patients choose

What patients check before they book, and where they give up

Your next patient usually starts with a need, not a name: a cracked tooth before a trip, a child's first checkup, a crown their insurance will cover. They search for that need, then weigh reviews, photos, opening hours and whether you take their plan.

Every one of those checks can end the visit before it starts. A page that stalls on a phone, hours that disagree with your profile, a treatment you offer but never mention: each sends the patient back to the results. We fix what patients see first, before we build anything new.

Many are choosing for someone else, too: a child, a parent or a partner. So we write pages that give the person reading enough confidence to book on another's behalf. Answer Step 1 and say which patients you would like more of.

Where patients give up

  1. A slow pageIt stalls on a phone and the patient taps back to the results.
  2. Hours that disagreeYour site says one thing and your profile another, and trust drops.
  3. An unanswered reviewA complaint left without a reply reads as a practice that does not care.

The channels

Dental marketing, channel by channel: the five we work on

Five pieces of digital marketing decide whether a patient finds your practice and books. We run them as one plan, because a strong treatment page next to a thin profile, or a good profile full of unanswered reviews, still loses the patient.

  1. 01

    Local SEO for every location

    Map results favor the practice nearest the searcher, so each location you run needs its own page and its own listing. We keep your practice name, address and phone number identical on every site that lists you, name the neighborhoods each location serves, and clean up duplicate listings that split your reviews. For a group with offices in several states, each location is planned for its own town. Answer Step 1 and we will list the places your details disagree.

  2. 02

    Google Business Profiles patients trust

    Your profile puts reviews, photos, hours and a booking link straight into the results. We choose the right primary category, fill in every field that applies to a dental office, swap stock pictures for photos of your own team and rooms, and answer the questions patients post, so the profile does the work of a first visit.

  3. 03

    A page for every treatment, general and specialty

    Implants, aligners, crowns, root canals and gum treatment are each searched by name. We write one page per treatment you want to grow, answer what patients ask about cost, comfort and recovery, and match every specialty word to the licenses your dentists actually hold.

  4. 04

    A website that turns visits into appointments

    Patients check insurance and payment before they commit. We put the plans you accept on every treatment page, keep the booking button within reach on a phone, make pages load quickly, and measure which pages lead to booked appointments. Design and conversion work are part of our Full Suite.

  5. 05

    Reviews and reputation

    Reviews are often the first thing a new patient reads about you. We set up a simple way to ask each patient after a visit, reply to every review without confirming the writer is a patient, and flag anything that needs a private follow-up. What patients praise most also tells us which treatment page to write next.

Your practice type

SEO for dental specialists and general dentists

Each kind of practice below is found through different searches, so your pages follow the treatments your own patients ask for. The same holds for practices limited to orofacial pain, oral medicine or dental anesthesiology. Answer Step 1 and tell us which kind you run.

  • Orthodontist SEO

    Families weigh braces for a teenager for months, while adults compare clear aligners on their own, often late at night. Orthodontist marketing has to speak to both, so each option you offer gets a page written for the person paying, and the consultation request comes to you.

  • Endodontist SEO

    Many root canal patients reach you on a referral, and many of them look you up before they book. We write calm treatment and retreatment pages that answer what worries them, plus a page for the dentists who send you patients.

  • Periodontist SEO

    People with gum trouble search for bleeding gums, receding gums or a deep cleaning, rarely for the word periodontist. We write your pages in their words, explain each treatment step plainly, and add implant pages if your practice places implants.

  • Pediatric dentist SEO

    Parents choose a children's dentist by feel: photos of the rooms, how the team talks about kids, what other parents say. We build the pages a parent reads before a first visit and keep your profile showing what that visit looks like.

  • Oral surgeon SEO

    Oral surgery searches start with a procedure: wisdom teeth, an extraction, a bone graft or an implant. We give each procedure you perform its own page covering preparation, recovery, eating afterwards and time off work or school, which is what patients read before they choose.

  • Prosthodontist SEO

    Patients researching dentures, implant-supported dentures or a full-mouth rebuild often read for weeks and bring a family member into the decision. We lay out each option on its own page, visit by visit, so your practice has answered their questions before they reach out.

  • Dental implant SEO

    Implant patients compare single-tooth and full-arch options and usually ask about cost first. Because a general dentist or a specialist may place implants, every page we write says which kind of dentist does the work at your practice.

  • Cosmetic dentist SEO

    Adults shopping for veneers, whitening, bonding or a smile makeover judge a practice by its before-and-after photos. We build your cosmetic pages around your own cases, shared with patient consent, because stock smiles convince nobody who is about to spend real money.

  • Emergency dentist SEO

    A broken tooth or a swollen jaw sends people to their phones for any dentist who can see them the same day. We build your urgent-care page and keep your profile's hours exact, holidays included, so a patient in pain finds an open door.

  • General and family dentist SEO

    Families want one dentist nearby who sees every age and has openings for new patients. Each service you offer, from cleanings to crowns, gets a page of its own, and where a service overlaps a specialty, the page says plainly that a general dentist provides it.

Your license first

Dental marketing that stays inside the rules

Your license is worth more than any ranking. The profession's code of ethics holds a dentist's website, and the search engine optimization behind it, to the same standard as any other announcement: truthful and not misleading. It also lets a general dentist announce any service, as long as the page says a general dentist provides it.

Each state's dental board adds rules of its own. In Nevada, for example, an ad may present a dentist as a specialist only with that specialty license and its number; the full Nevada rules are on Dental SEO in Nevada. We write every state's pages inside its own rules. Begin with Step 1 and we will review your current wording.

What is local SEO for a dental practice?

It is the part of dental SEO that works on the map results and on searches with a place in them, such as a dentist near me. It rests on your Business Profile, your reviews, a page for each location and the same practice details everywhere you are listed, and it is usually where we start.

What does dental marketing cover, and what does a first page guarantee promise a practice?

Dental marketing covers every way a patient finds and chooses you: search, your Business Profile, reviews, referrals and your website. Our first page guarantee is about where your practice ranks, never a promise of new patients. Around it we build the treatment pages, profile work and review requests that turn a search into a booking, and our Full Suite takes care of your website design and conversion.

Do you write pages for orthodontists, endodontists and other specialists?

Yes. Each specialty gets pages built around its own procedures and the words its patients use, and no page presents a dentist as a specialist without the license to match. If referrals bring you patients, we add a page for the dentists who send them.

My state is not on your list yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Tell us where you are in Step 1. Before we suggest anything, we look at who holds the first page for your treatments there and read your state board's advertising rules.

How do referrals from other dentists fit into search?

Specialty patients often arrive on a referral, and many still look you up before they book. A clear page about how you work with referring dentists reassures both the patient and the dentist who sent them, and it can lead other dentists to refer to you as well.

Do you handle practices with several locations?

Yes. Each location needs its own page, its own Business Profile and its own reviews, because patients search for the location nearest them. We keep your name, hours and services consistent across all of them while each page speaks for its own neighborhood and team.
