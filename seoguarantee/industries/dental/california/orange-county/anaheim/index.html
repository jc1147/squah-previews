Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved to Anaheim in a typical year (at least) † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys. Moves from elsewhere in Orange County are not in the survey table, so the figure is a floor.

  • Dentists with a practice address in the city (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry with a practice address in the Anaheim ZIP codes was placed on the map; the count keeps those inside the 2025 city boundary, so it is a floor, and it includes teaching faculty and residents, not only dentists in private practice.

  • Dentists with an Anaheim address outside the city † is derived: the same records whose practice address says Anaheim but falls outside the city boundary, counted by the California city or census place each falls in.

  • Dentists in the city by main field † is derived: the dentists inside the city limits, counted by the main field each lists in NPPES (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Average receipts per dental office † is derived: Anaheim dental offices' 2022 receipts divided by the number of Anaheim dental offices.

  • Residents per dental office † is derived: the city's July 2022 population estimate divided by the 2022 count of dental offices in the city.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.

  • ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct (2026): section 5.I (version dated 2026-02-18).

  • California Business and Professions Code, section 1680: BPC 1680.
