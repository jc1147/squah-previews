Your patients here search for exactly what they want: a Saturday cleaning in Tustin, an Irvine dentist on their PPO plan, an emergency visit in Garden Grove. Reviews, photos and hours get compared, and many book online before phoning anyone.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, 2025 annual averages.

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved into Orange County in a typical year † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another California county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Dentists by city † is derived: the county's dentist records, counted by the city each practice address falls in.

  • Dentists with a practice address in Orange County (at least) † is derived: every active dentist in the NPPES registry whose practice address places inside Orange County; a floor that also counts faculty and residents.

  • Dentists in Orange County by main field † is derived: the county's dentists counted by the main field each lists (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Residents per dental office † is derived: the county's July 2022 population estimate divided by the 2022 count of dental offices in the county.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.

Rules cited on this page

  • California Code of Regulations, title 16, division 10 (Dental Board of California), article 7 (Advertising): 16 CCR 1052.

  • Code of Federal Regulations, title 16, part 465 (FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials): 16 CFR 465.2.

  • ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct (2026): section 5.I (version dated 2026-02-18).

  • California Business and Professions Code, section 1680: BPC 1680.

  • California Business and Professions Code, section 651: BPC 651.
