How patients choose

How patients choose a plastic surgeon, and where they drop off

Your next patient may spend weeks deciding. They look through before-and-after galleries, read reviews, check who will operate and what the recovery takes, and many book a consultation with more than one surgeon before they commit.

Every step is a chance to lose them. A gallery that loads slowly on a phone, a procedure you perform but never describe, a consultation form that asks too much too soon: each one sends the patient back to the search results. We fix what patients see first, then build what is missing.

Many also want privacy while they research, so your pages should answer the awkward questions plainly, from what shapes the cost to how long they will be off work. Tell us in Step 1 which procedures you want more consultations for.

Where patients drop off

  1. A slow galleryPhotos that take seconds to load on a phone lose the reader.
  2. A missing procedure pageIf you offer it but never explain it, patients assume you do not.
  3. A thin profileFew photos and old reviews make a practice look quiet.

  5. 05

    Reviews and galleries carry a lot of weight in this field. We make it easy to invite a review once recovery is over, reply to each one without confirming the writer was a patient, and use only your own patients' photos, shared with their consent.

Your practice type

Plastic surgery SEO by procedure

Patients search by procedure, so your pages should follow the procedures you perform. Tell us in Step 1 which of them matter most to your practice.

  • Breast surgery SEO

    Augmentation, lifts, reductions and implant changes are each researched on their own, often for months. We give every breast procedure you perform a page that explains the options, the consultation and the recovery, and shows your own results with patient consent.

  • Facelift and neck lift SEO

    Facial surgery patients want to know how natural the result will look and how long they will stay out of sight. We write pages that answer both, set out the difference between the procedures you offer, and keep your gallery organized by procedure.

  • Rhinoplasty SEO

    People considering nose surgery often have a breathing problem as well as a cosmetic concern. We write your rhinoplasty pages to cover both reasons, explain revision surgery if you perform it, and answer what patients ask about swelling and time off work.

  • Eyelid surgery SEO

    Eyelid surgery draws patients who want to look less tired and some whose sight is affected. We build a page that explains upper and lower eyelid procedures in plain words and says clearly when your consultation includes a vision check, if yours does.

  • Tummy tuck SEO

    Many tummy tuck patients are parents or people who have lost a lot of weight. We write for both, explain how the procedure differs from liposuction, and describe recovery honestly, without promising a shape or a size.

  • Liposuction SEO

    Liposuction is widely misunderstood. Your page should say what it can change and what it cannot, make clear that it is not a weight-loss treatment, and show who is a good candidate, so the patients who book arrive with the right expectations.

  • Mommy makeover SEO

    Patients planning several procedures in one surgery research the combination as a whole. We build a page that explains what can be combined at your practice, how recovery is planned around family life and what the consultation covers.

  • Body contouring after weight loss SEO

    Patients who have lost a great deal of weight look for surgeons who remove loose skin and reshape the body. We give arm, thigh and lower body lifts their own pages and speak plainly about what surgery can and cannot do.

  • Reconstructive surgery SEO

    Breast reconstruction, scar revision and repair after an injury are searched by people facing hard decisions. We write these pages in a calm tone, explain what insurance may cover where that applies at your practice, and keep them apart from your cosmetic pages.

  • Injectables and non-surgical SEO

    Wrinkle-relaxing injections, fillers and skin treatments bring in patients who may choose surgery later. We describe each treatment by what it does, never by brand name, and say who on your team performs it.

  • Male plastic surgery SEO

    Men considering surgery often search quietly and read more before they reach out. We give procedures such as eyelid surgery, liposuction and chest reduction pages written with them in mind, with a gallery of male patients if you have one.

Claims you can stand behind

Plastic surgery marketing inside the advertising rules

Your license and your reputation are worth more than any ranking. Federal rules treat your before-and-after photos and patient stories as endorsements: a result you show is read as typical unless the page clearly says what patients generally achieve, and a line such as results not typical does not fix a dramatic claim. Reviews must come from real patients, never bought or written for them.

State medical boards add advertising rules of their own, including how surgeons may describe their training and credentials, and we read your state's before we write a word. Start at Step 1, and the claims on your current pages get checked against them.

What is included in plastic surgery marketing, and what exactly does the first page guarantee cover?

It covers every step from the first search to the consultation request: your Business Profile, reviews, galleries, procedure pages and the website that ties them together. The first page guarantee is about rankings for your procedures, never a promise of new patients or booked surgeries; the rest of the work turns those rankings into consultations.

How do before-and-after photos fit into SEO?

They are often the pages patients spend longest on. We use only your own patients' photos, shared with written consent, label each one with the procedure, and keep the words beside them honest about how results vary from one patient to the next.

Do you build a page for each procedure?

Yes, for every procedure you want to grow. Patients tend to look up the procedure before they look for a surgeon, so each page explains what it involves at your practice, who performs it and how recovery goes.

Can you help if patients travel to see me?

Yes. We add pages for the towns and states your patients come from and answer what a traveling patient needs to plan: consultations by video if you offer them, how long to stay nearby and when to come back for follow-up.

Can you work with a practice in any state?

Yes. Tell us your state in Step 1. We read its medical board's advertising rules and see which practices already rank for your procedures there before we suggest a plan.
