How patients choose

How a patient picks a chiropractor, and where clinics lose them

Your next patient usually starts with a problem, not a name: a stiff neck after a long drive, a sore back from a new job, a runner's knee before race season. They search for that problem or for a kind of care they have heard about, weigh the reviews and photos they find, and check that you accept their insurance.

Before booking, many want to know what happens at a first visit, whether X-rays are part of it and how many visits a plan usually runs. When a site leaves those questions open, the patient moves on to one that answers them. We put the answers on your pages in plain words, without promises your evidence cannot back.

Parents choosing for a child, and adults booking for an older parent, read even more closely. Name the patients you want more of in Step 1; we build the pages they read first.

Where clinics lose the patient

  1. A page that hides the basicsNo word on the first visit, insurance or cost, so the patient keeps looking.
  2. Hours that are wrongThe profile says open, the door is locked, and the next review says so.
  3. Silence on reviewsAn unanswered complaint tells a new patient that nobody is listening.

The channels

Chiropractic digital marketing: the five channels we run for your clinic

A patient finds and books a clinic through five channels, and a weak one undoes the others: a strong page beside an empty profile, or a full profile with unanswered reviews, still sends the patient elsewhere. So we run all five together.

  1. 01

    Local SEO for each clinic you run

    The map favors the clinic closest to the person searching, so every location you run needs a page and a listing of its own. We make your clinic's name, address and phone number match wherever it is listed, name the neighborhoods and towns each location draws from, and merge duplicate listings that split your reviews in two. A group with clinics in several cities gets a plan for each city. Begin with Step 1, and checking where your listings disagree comes first.

  2. 02

    A Business Profile that answers first

    Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a patient sees: reviews, photos, hours and a way to book. We set the right primary category, list each service you offer, swap stock images for photos of your doctors, front desk and treatment rooms, keep holiday hours current, and answer the questions people post, so the profile earns the first visit.

  3. 03

    A page for each kind of care

    Sports injuries, auto-accident care, pregnancy care, decompression and family visits each draw searches of their own. For every kind of care you want to grow, we write a page that covers the first visit, the usual plan and the cost, and describes credentials and techniques only in words your state board accepts.

  4. 04

    A website that books the first visit

    A patient ready to book wants the insurance you take, your next opening and a button they can reach with a thumb. We put those on every service page, keep pages quick on a phone, and track which pages lead to booked first visits. Site design and conversion work come with our Full Suite.

  5. 05

    Reviews and reputation

    New patients read what earlier patients say before they read anything you wrote. We set up a simple request after each visit, answer every review in a way that never confirms the reviewer was treated at your clinic, and never write, buy or invent a review. The praise you collect also shows us which page to write next.

Your practice type

Chiropractic SEO shaped around the care you give

Patients rarely search for a chiropractor in general; they look for help with something specific, and every kind of practice below has searches of its own. Start with Step 1 and say which kind of care you want to grow.

  • Sports chiropractor SEO

    Weekend athletes and high school teams look for a chiropractor who understands their game, and they judge by whether your site speaks it. We build pages around the sports and injuries you see most, explain the care each involves, and mention a sports credential only where your state board permits it.

  • Chiropractic rehab SEO

    People coming back from a sprain, a strain or weeks off work search for a plan to get moving again, often without typing the word chiropractor. We describe each rehab program you run, visit by visit, and name only the therapies your license covers where you practice.

  • Pediatric chiropractor SEO

    A parent deciding whether to bring a child to a chiropractor wants to see the room, meet the doctor and understand every step first. We write gentle, plain pages about how you care for young patients, show your space in real photos, and leave out any claim about treating childhood conditions.

  • Chiropractic neurology SEO

    Patients who find chiropractic neurology have usually read a great deal and arrive with pointed questions about the assessment. Our pages walk through how your exam works and how you track progress, state your training in the terms your board allows, and make no promise about outcomes.

  • Personal injury chiropractic SEO

    After a crash or a fall at work, people look for a chiropractor who can see them soon and keep careful records for an insurer or an attorney. We write pages about your exam, your records and your scheduling, and we never reach out to accident victims, something several states restrict.

  • Spinal decompression SEO

    Decompression gets researched hard: people want the price of a course, the number of sessions and what the table actually is. We answer each of those honestly, describe your equipment as federal device rules classify it, and never promise relief.

  • Prenatal chiropractor SEO

    Expecting parents choose a prenatal chiropractor with care, reading about tables, positions and at what stage of pregnancy visits can begin. We write each page in a calm, exact tone, show the setup you really use, and say nothing about labor or delivery outcomes.

  • Upper cervical chiropractor SEO

    People loyal to upper cervical care often pick the technique before the clinic, and some will drive a long way for it. We give your technique, your imaging and your usual care plan their own clear pages so those patients find you, and keep every health claim to what your evidence supports.

  • Wellness and corrective care chiropractor SEO

    Patients weighing a corrective or maintenance plan want the schedule and the cost spelled out before they commit. We present your plans exactly as they run, state visit counts and fees accurately, use no fear about skipping care, and show reviews only from real patients.

  • General and family chiropractor SEO

    Most families want one chiropractor close to home who sees every age and can fit them in this week. We keep your map listing and hours exact, give each service its own page, and title every doctor as a doctor of chiropractic throughout your site.

Rules before rankings

Chiropractic marketing written inside the rules

No ranking is worth a complaint to your board, so the rules come first on every page we write. Federal rules apply in every state: a review must come from a real patient describing their own visit, and a patient's story of a good result is read as the norm, so we show it only beside what patients generally experience. Federal rules also classify powered traction equipment as a medical device, so we describe decompression equipment as the device it is, never as FDA approved.

Each state's chiropractic board adds its own rules. Arizona, for example, counts the words specialist, specializing and expert in a chiropractic ad as misleading; the Arizona rules in full are on Chiropractic SEO in Arizona. Share your website address in Step 1; we read your current pages against your own board's rules.

How is chiropractic marketing different from SEO, and what exactly is guaranteed on the first page?

SEO is the part that decides where your clinic shows up when people search. Chiropractic marketing is wider: your Business Profile, reviews, referrals from other providers and a website that turns a visit into a booking. What we guarantee is a first page ranking for your clinic, never a number of new patients. The profile, page and review work we do alongside it is what turns that ranking into a first visit, and our Full Suite covers design and conversion.

Can my website describe me as a specialist in sports or pediatric chiropractic?

That depends on your state. Some boards bar those words in a chiropractic ad outright, as Arizona does, and others allow a credential only when they recognize it or when the certifying body is named. We check your state's rule before any credential goes on a page, and we describe the care you give rather than lean on a title.

Do you market clinics that treat car accident and work injury patients?

Yes. We write pages that explain your exam, how you keep records for an insurer or attorney and how soon you can see someone, and we make sure your hours and location are right on the map. We never contact accident victims for you: Arizona, for one, treats soliciting a person hurt in a crash within the previous fifteen days as grounds for discipline, and other states restrict it as well.

My clinic is outside the five states on this page. Can you still help?

Yes. Tell us in Step 1 where you practice. We then check which clinics already hold page one for your kind of care in your area, and read your chiropractic board's advertising rules, before recommending anything.
