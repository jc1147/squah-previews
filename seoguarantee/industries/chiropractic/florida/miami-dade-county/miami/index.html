    During pregnancy, patients look for a chiropractor they can trust with a changing body, and they check reviews, photos of the tables you use and who treats them. Prenatal chiropractic marketing means a page for each stage you care for and your Business Profile with real photos, without claims about labor or delivery.

  • Upper cervical chiropractor SEO

    Upper cervical chiropractic can draw patients from well beyond the nearest few blocks, since many look for the technique first and the distance second. We build pages and your Business Profile that name your technique and explain your process, so people willing to drive find you, with no promises about what care will fix.

  • Wellness and corrective care chiropractor SEO

Family chiropractic marketing puts your hours, services and reviews where they look, gives each service a page, and makes no claim that you beat other chiropractors nearby.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved to Miami in a typical year (at least) † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a move from elsewhere in Miami-Dade County is not counted, so it is a floor; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Chiropractors in the city by main field † is derived: the chiropractors inside the city limits, counted by the main field each lists (self-reported, not a license or board certification).

  • Chiropractors with a practice address in the city (at least) † is derived: every active chiropractor in the NPPES registry with a practice address in the Miami ZIP codes was placed on the map; the count keeps those inside the 2025 city boundary, so it is a floor.

  • Chiropractors with a Miami address outside the city † is derived: the same records whose practice address says Miami but falls outside the city boundary, counted by the Florida city or census place each falls in.

  • Average receipts per chiropractic office † is derived: Miami chiropractic offices' 2022 receipts divided by the number of those offices.

  • Residents per chiropractic office † is derived: Miami's July 2022 population estimate divided by the 2022 count of chiropractic offices with paid staff.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.

Rules cited on this page

  • Code of Federal Regulations, title 16, part 465 (FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials): 16 CFR 465.2.

  • Code of Federal Regulations, title 21, part 890 (FDA: Physical Medicine Devices): 21 CFR 890.5900.

  • Florida Administrative Code, rule chapter 64B2-15 (Board of Chiropractic Medicine): FAC 64B2-15.001.

  • Florida Statutes, chapter 460 (Chiropractic Medicine): FS 460.413(1).
