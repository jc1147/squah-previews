Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census; 2025 TIGER/Line city boundaries; Census Geocoder (to place each practice address on the map).

  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, 2025 annual averages.

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): NPPES NPI Registry, September 26, 2026.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved into Clark County in a typical year † is derived: residents who said they lived in another Nevada county, another state or abroad one year earlier, added up; the survey averages 2020 to 2024, so this is a typical year.

  • Chiropractors in Clark County by main field † is derived: the chiropractors with a practice address in Clark County, counted by the main field each lists in NPPES (self-reported, not a license count).

  • Chiropractors by city or community † is derived: the same Clark County chiropractors, counted by the city or unincorporated community each practice address falls in, using the 2025 census boundaries.

  • Chiropractors with a practice address in Clark County (at least) † is derived: every active chiropractor in the NPPES registry whose main practice address in Nevada was placed on the map inside Clark County; addresses the map could not place are left out, so it is a floor, and it includes teaching faculty and residents.

  • Change in residents, July 2024 to July 2025 † is derived: Clark County's population estimate for July 1, 2025 minus its estimate for July 1, 2024.

  • Average receipts per chiropractic office † is derived: all 2022 receipts of Clark County's chiropractic offices with paid staff divided by the number of those offices.

Rules cited on this page

  • Code of Federal Regulations, title 16, part 465 (FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials): 16 CFR 465.2.

  • Code of Federal Regulations, title 21, part 890 (FDA: Physical Medicine Devices): 21 CFR 890.5900.

  • Nevada Administrative Code, chapter 634 (Chiropractic Physicians’ Board of Nevada): NAC 634.521, NAC 634.536, NAC 634.550 (version dated 2026-04-13).

  • Nevada Revised Statutes, chapter 634 (Chiropractic Physicians): NRS 634.018 (version dated 2026-04-21).
