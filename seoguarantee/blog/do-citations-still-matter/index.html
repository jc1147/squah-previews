Do Local Citations Still Matter in 2026?

Citations are now a small map-pack factor and a bigger AI factor. What Whitespark's 2026 survey, Yext and BrightLocal found, which listings matter, and how to clean up inconsistent ones.

Yes, but less for the map pack and more for AI answers. The local search experts Whitespark surveyed for 2026 weight citations at 6% of map-pack rankings and 13% of AI search visibility, and say three of the top five AI visibility factors are citation factors [1]. Keep your core listings accurate everywhere, then earn mentions on the sites customers and AI tools trust.

The short answer

Direct answer

Citations still matter, in a narrower way. For the map pack they are a maintenance item: 6% of Whitespark's 2026 local pack weighting, with the best-rated citation factor only 28th [1]. For AI answers they matter more: 13% of AI visibility in the same survey [1], and listings supplied 42% of the 6.8 million AI citations Yext counted [2]. Accuracy beats quantity.

Google's own ranking page stopped mentioning directories in 2025. The older version said prominence drew on information "from across the web, like links, articles, and directories" [3]. The current page names links and reviews instead: how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have [4].

Stat

Whitespark's 2026 survey weights citations at 6% of local pack rankings, 7% of local organic rankings and 13% of AI search visibility [1].

For the map pack the trend is down. Whitespark reports that citations "lost about half a percentage point" from the previous edition, continuing a downward trend [1]. For AI visibility, the same report puts it bluntly: "In AI SEO, mentions (citations) are the new link." [1]

What a citation is (and NAP)

Direct answer

A local citation is any online mention of your business's name, address and phone number, known together as NAP. Structured citations sit in listing fields on directories and data aggregators; unstructured citations are mentions in newspaper articles, blog posts, government sites or industry associations [1]. NAP consistency means every one of them matches your real business, down to the suite number.

Google asks businesses to represent themselves as they are "consistently represented and recognized in the real world" across signage, stationery and other branding [5]. That is the standard to hold every listing to.

Your own website counts too. Whitespark's experts rank HTML NAP matching your Business Profile 15th among local pack factors, rank the absence of crawlable NAP on your website 10th among negative factors, and rank mismatched NAP or tracking phone numbers across the data ecosystem 24th among negatives [1]. Service-area businesses are a special case: Google tells them to hide their address from customers [5], so their listings should make the same choice.

Where experts rank citations today

Direct answer

Low for the map pack, higher for AI. In Whitespark's 2026 survey, the best-rated citation factor for the local pack, consistency of citations on Google Maps, Bing Maps and Apple Maps, ranks 28th, and the sheer quantity of structured citations ranks 110th [1]. For AI search visibility, Whitespark says three of the top five factors are citation factors [1].

Here is how the 2026 survey splits the weight across all eight factor groups [1]:

Read it as expert opinion: 47 practitioners scored 187 factors, and none has special access to Google's algorithm [1]. Two things stand out. In the map pack, citations sit near the bottom of the list. For AI visibility, they tie with links and trail only on-page signals and reviews. Citations are neither dead nor a leading map factor; they are a foundation that now does double duty.

Citations and AI answers

Direct answer

AI tools lean on listings. Yext's study of 6.8 million AI citations across Gemini, OpenAI and Perplexity found listings supplied 42% and websites 44%, with listings rising to 52.6% in healthcare [2]. In a small BrightLocal test, Yelp was a source in 33% of searches [6]. Yext sells listings software and BrightLocal's sample was small, so treat both as directional.

Stat

Yext found 86% of the AI citations it analyzed came from sources brands already manage, such as their own websites and listings; Reddit supplied just 2% [2].

The engines differ. In Yext's data, Gemini favored websites (52.1%) while OpenAI leaned on listings (48.7%) [7]. BrightLocal's test, 20 searches across 10 niches in 4 AI tools, found that in its dental searches ChatGPT sourced its information only from dental directories, ten of them [6].

What the platforms say fits that picture. OpenAI says ChatGPT uses trusted third-party providers for local information such as nearby business listings, without naming them [8]. Google's Gemini app answers place questions with public information from Google Maps [9]. Microsoft tells local businesses to register with Bing Places for Business so their address, hours and contact details stay eligible for AI answers [10].

The mentions that matter most are earned. Whitespark's top four AI visibility factors are presence on expert-curated best-of lists, a dedicated page for each service, prominence on key industry domains, and the quality of unstructured citations such as news articles, government sites and industry associations [1]. More on the channel side in our guides to getting recommended by ChatGPT and AI search optimization.

Which listings matter for your industry

Direct answer

Start with the platforms that feed maps and AI: your Google Business Profile, Bing Maps and Apple Maps, the three named in Whitespark's best-rated citation factor [1]. Then the major directories customers read, such as Yelp, then your industry's own directories and associations, then earned mentions on local news sites, best-of lists and industry sites [1][6].

Google's own guide to generative AI search says it plainly: "Using products like Merchant Center (such as Merchant Center feeds) and Google Business Profiles can help your products and services to be visible in both AI responses and other Google Search results." [11] Our Google Business Profile optimization guide covers that profile in depth.

Industry platforms vary. For restaurants, OpenAI says ChatGPT may show reservation availability from OpenTable, Resy or Yelp [8]. For healthcare, Yext found listings carried more than half of AI citations [2], so the medical directories patients use deserve the same care as Google.

Quality beats quantity, and fakes backfire. Google's AI guide warns that seeking inauthentic "mentions" across the web isn't as helpful as it might seem [11], and its spam policies list "low-quality directory or bookmark site links" as link spam [12]. Listing your business on directories customers actually use is housekeeping; buying hundreds of junk listings is not.

Cleaning up inconsistent listings

Direct answer

Fix the source first, then the copies. Settle your exact name, address and phone number on your website and Google Business Profile. Then search for every variant you have ever used, including old addresses, old numbers and tracking numbers, and correct or close each listing, starting with the maps and the biggest directories. Keep one record of every listing and recheck it.

Duplicates deserve special attention. Google's rule is one profile per business, and your business name should be the real-world name, with no added keywords, locations or taglines, which can get a profile suspended [5].

Frequently asked questions

What is a local citation?

A local citation is an online mention of your business's name, address and phone number. Structured citations are listings on directories and data aggregators; unstructured citations are mentions in news articles, blog posts, government sites or industry associations [1]. Both tell search engines and AI tools where your business is and how to reach it, and consistent details make that signal easier to trust.

Do citations still help rankings?

A little for the map pack, more for AI answers. Whitespark's 2026 survey weights citations at 6% of local pack rankings and 13% of AI search visibility [1]. Google's ranking page no longer mentions directories, though its older version did [3][4]. Keep the core listings accurate, and don't expect a flood of new listings to move your map rankings.

How many citations does a business need?

There is no magic number, and quantity is not what the experts value. Whitespark's experts rank the quantity of structured citations 110th among local pack factors, while consistency on Google Maps, Bing Maps and Apple Maps ranks 28th [1]. Cover the platforms your customers and AI tools use, match what the businesses already in the map results for your phrases have, and keep every listing accurate.

What is NAP consistency?

NAP consistency means your business name, address and phone number read the same everywhere: your website, your Google Business Profile and every listing. Google asks businesses to appear as they are consistently represented in the real world [5], and Whitespark's experts count mismatched NAP or tracking phone numbers across the data ecosystem among negative factors [1]. Pick one exact version and use it everywhere.

Do AI assistants use business listings?

By the available evidence, yes. Yext found listings supplied 42% of the AI citations it analyzed [2]. OpenAI says ChatGPT uses third-party providers for nearby business listings [8], Google's Gemini app answers place questions from Google Maps [9], and Microsoft tells local businesses to register with Bing Places for AI answers [10]. Accurate listings are the raw material for those answers.
