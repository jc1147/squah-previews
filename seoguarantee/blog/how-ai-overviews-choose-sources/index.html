How AI Overviews Choose Sources (and How to Rank in Them)

How Google decides which pages an AI Overview links to, what Google says you need to be eligible, what third-party citation studies found, and how local businesses show up.

AI Overviews choose sources by retrieving pages from Google's search index with its core ranking systems, sometimes after running several related searches, and linking the pages that support the answer. To rank in AI Overviews, a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet; beyond that, Google says there are no special requirements [1][2]. This article covers when AI Overviews appear, how Google picks the links, what third-party studies found, and what it means for a local business.

The short answer

Direct answer

Google builds an AI Overview by retrieving pages from its index with its core ranking systems and grounding the answer in them, sometimes after running several related searches, which it calls query fan-out. Any page that is indexed and eligible for a snippet can be linked. Studies disagree on how often those links come from the organic top ten, so rank well and give the answer something unique to cite.

What an AI Overview is, and when it appears

Direct answer

An AI Overview is the AI-written summary, with supporting links, that Google shows on some results pages. Google launched them to everyone in the US on May 14, 2024, and shows them only when its systems judge them additive. Measures differ: Semrush found them on 15.69% of the queries it tracked in November 2025, and Advanced Web Ranking on 43% of sampled US desktop results pages in July 2026.

Google is direct about it: "AI Overviews are only shown when our systems determine that it is additive to classic Search, and as such, often don't trigger." [2]

Eligibility: indexed and snippet-eligible

Direct answer

Google's rule is short: to be shown as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode, a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet, and there are no additional technical requirements. The site also must not be excluded from Search's generative AI features in Search Console, where sites are included by default.

In Google's words: "To be eligible to be shown as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode, a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet, fulfilling the Search technical requirements. There are no additional technical requirements." [2] Eligibility is not a promise, though: "Indexing and serving isn't guaranteed." [2]

Query fan-out and why cited pages vary

Direct answer

Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode may use query fan-out: the model issues several related searches across subtopics and data sources, then builds one answer from the results. Google says this lets it link a wider and more diverse set of pages than a classic web search. Ahrefs links fan-out to the falling overlap between AI Overview citations and the top ten.

Google defines fan-out as "A set of concurrent, related queries generated by the model to request more information and fetch additional relevant search results to address the user's query." [1] The retrieval itself relies on "our core Search ranking systems to retrieve relevant, up-to-date web pages from our Search index." [1] In practice, a page can be pulled in because it answers one of the related searches, not only the one the user typed. That is our reading of Google's description, not a Google statement.

Cited pages also shift between products and runs. Ahrefs found only 13.7% of citations overlapped between AI Overviews and AI Mode across 730,000 response pairs [9]. SE Ranking ran the same AI Mode searches three times and found only 9.2% of URLs matched across all three [10].

One warning. Google says that creating pages for each fan-out query "primarily to manipulate rankings or generative AI responses in Google Search violates Google's scaled content abuse spam policy." [1] Cover the natural subtopics on one strong page instead.

What studies found about cited pages

Direct answer

The studies disagree, so treat any single number with care. Estimates of how many AI Overview citations also rank in the organic top ten run from 16.7% (BrightEdge, September 2025) to 76% (Ahrefs, July 2025), and Ahrefs' March 2026 update put it near 38%. Every study finds overlap with organic rankings; none agrees on how much.

Which sites get cited most? Pew found that Wikipedia, YouTube and Reddit together accounted for 15% of the sources listed in AI summaries [3], and Ahrefs calls YouTube the most cited domain in AI Overviews today [14]. Mentions matter too: in Ahrefs' study of 75K brands, web mentions correlated with AI Overview visibility at 0.664, against 0.218 for backlinks [15].

Key takeaway

Every study finds overlap between AI Overview citations and organic rankings, but none agrees on how much, so work on the page and on what the rest of the web says about you.

Local businesses in AI Overviews

Direct answer

Google says AI responses can include information about local businesses, and that an up-to-date Business Profile helps. In our own check from Las Vegas, only 2 of 12 commercial searches showed an AI Overview, and neither named a business from the map results, while the map results showed on 11 of the 12. Samples differ, so check your own market.

Google's guide says: "Where appropriate, generative AI responses can include product listings, product information, and information about local businesses." [1] Its advice for AI features includes keeping "Merchant Center and Business Profile information" up to date [16].

What the checks show:

  • In our Las Vegas check on September 27, 2026, the two AI Overviews appeared for "dental implants" and "botox". Both were informational, and the "dental implants" one linked several dental practice websites outside Las Vegas [6].
  • In a US-wide capture for "near me seo" the same day, the AI Overview named two of the three map-pack businesses with their star ratings and review counts, without linking to them. It is a single observation, but it shows profile data surfacing in an answer [6].
  • Whitespark reported AI Overviews on an average of 68% of the local searches in its sample, without publishing its method [17].

The practical steps are the local basics: a complete, accurate Google Business Profile, genuine reviews, and service pages that answer the questions your customers ask.

How to measure your AI Overview presence

Direct answer

Search Console includes AI Overview traffic in the Performance report under "Web" and counts each AI Overview as a single position shared by all its links. Since June 2026 it also offers a generative AI report of impressions. For citations on specific searches, capture the results page from your own market on a schedule, because AI Overviews vary by query, place and time.

Search Console's counting rules: "An AI Overview occupies a single position in search results", and all links in it share that position [18]. The generative AI report launched on June 3, 2026, reached all sites by August 31, 2026, and reports impressions; its help page does not mention clicks [19][20].

Expect fewer clicks per impression where AI Overviews appear. Pew found users clicked a regular result on 8% of visits with an AI summary, against 15% without one [3]. Ahrefs measured a 58% lower click-through rate for the top-ranking page when an AI Overview was present [21]. Cited brands fare better: Seer Interactive found brands earned 120% more organic clicks per impression when cited than when they were not [22]. Google disputes reports of dramatic declines, calling organic click volume "relatively stable" [23]. More in our organic click-through rate article.

Our first page guarantee counts Google's AI answer as one of its three places: your Google Business Profile in the map results, page one of the organic results, and AI suggestions and answers. Under the written guarantee terms, the AI place is the answer written by AI that Google shows on the results page, when it names or links your business, and the share is measured separately in each of the three places. Because AI Overviews appear on only some searches, the terms define how the AI share is measured.

Frequently asked questions

How does Google choose sources for AI Overviews?

Google retrieves relevant, up-to-date pages from its index using its core ranking systems, sometimes after running several related searches (query fan-out), and grounds the answer in them [1]. A page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet to be linked [2]. Google adds that a unique viewpoint that stands out can help, because its systems look at a variety of sources [1].

Do I need special markup to appear in AI Overviews?

No. Google says: "There's also no special schema.org structured data that you need to add." [2] You also don't need AI text files or new markup, and Google Search ignores llms.txt [1]. Structured data still helps with rich results, but it is not a ticket into AI Overviews.

Why don't AI Overviews show for every search?

Google shows them only when its systems judge them additive to classic Search, so they "often don't trigger" [2]. Pew found them on 18% of searches in March 2025, with long, question-style searches far more likely to get one [3]. In our Las Vegas check, 2 of 12 commercial searches showed an AI Overview [6].

Can a local business appear in an AI Overview?

Yes. Google says AI responses can include information about local businesses, and that an up-to-date Business Profile helps [1][16]. In a US-wide capture for "near me seo", the AI Overview named two map-pack businesses with their ratings [6]. In our Las Vegas check, though, 10 of the 12 commercial searches had no AI Overview, while 11 showed the map results [6].

Do AI Overviews reduce clicks?

Third-party studies say yes on the searches where they appear. Pew measured clicks on regular results on 8% of visits with an AI summary, against 15% without one, and Ahrefs estimated a 58% lower click-through rate for the top result [3][21]. Google disputes reports of dramatic declines [23]. Seer found brands earned more clicks per impression when cited than when not [22].

How do I track AI Overview citations?

Use Search Console's Performance report, where AI Overview traffic sits under "Web", and its generative AI report for impressions [2][19]. Neither lists citations query by query, so capture results pages for your key searches from your own market on a schedule and record whether an AI Overview appears and whether it names or links you.

Earn the citation the ordinary way

AI Overviews reward the same things Google's regular results do: indexable pages, useful and specific answers, and a business the rest of the web talks about.
