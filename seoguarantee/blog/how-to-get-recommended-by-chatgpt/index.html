How to Get Your Business Recommended by ChatGPT

To get your business recommended by ChatGPT, let OpenAI's search crawler reach your site, keep your business listed accurately on the sites ChatGPT draws on, and earn the reviews and mentions that make you a credible answer. OpenAI says any public website can appear in ChatGPT search, and also that placement is not guaranteed [1][2]. Here is what OpenAI actually says, what third-party studies found, and the steps you control.

The short answer

Direct answer

ChatGPT SEO rests on three things. Be findable: allow OAI-SearchBot, OpenAI's search crawler, and make sure your host doesn't block it. Be consistent: keep your name, hours and services identical on your site and the listings AI tools read. Be reviewed and mentioned: SOCi found ChatGPT recommends few locations, and the ones it picks are well rated.

How ChatGPT search finds businesses, in OpenAI's own words

Direct answer

OpenAI launched ChatGPT search on October 31, 2024. When it searches, ChatGPT typically rewrites your question into one or more targeted queries, sometimes through partner search providers, and ranks results using multiple factors meant to surface relevant, reliable information. For local questions it can use an approximate location and third-party listing providers it doesn't name.

OpenAI's help page is short and worth reading closely [2]:

  • ChatGPT search "sometimes partners with other search providers," and the page links Microsoft's and Shopify's privacy statements for those providers.
  • It "typically rewrites your query into one or more targeted queries".
  • It ranks results "using multiple factors intended to help users find relevant, reliable information," and adds: "Placement is not guaranteed."
  • For local results, ChatGPT "may use an approximate location based on your IP address," and it uses third-party providers for things like nearby business listings; for restaurants, it may show availability from OpenTable, Resy or Yelp.

OpenAI's publisher FAQ adds the good news: "Any public website can appear in ChatGPT search." [1] What OpenAI doesn't say matters too. It doesn't name its business-listing providers, so claims that ChatGPT reads your Google Business Profile are unsourced.

OAI-SearchBot vs GPTBot: the setting that decides eligibility

Direct answer

OpenAI runs separate crawlers with separate jobs. OAI-SearchBot gathers pages for ChatGPT search; GPTBot collects content for model training. OpenAI says you can allow OAI-SearchBot to appear in search while disallowing GPTBot. Blocking GPTBot does not remove you from ChatGPT search; opting out of OAI-SearchBot keeps your pages out of its search answers.

Three details from OpenAI worth acting on:

  • A robots.txt change can take roughly 24 hours to take effect for its crawlers [3].
  • Your host or CDN must let OpenAI's published IP addresses through, not just your robots.txt [2].
  • A page blocked in robots.txt may still appear in ChatGPT Atlas as a bare link and title unless it carries noindex [1].

The sources it leans on: listings, reviews, websites

Direct answer

OpenAI doesn't publish which listing providers it uses, but vendor studies give a picture. Yext found OpenAI's models leaned on listings for 48.7% of local citations. In BrightLocal's small test, ChatGPT drew its dental information exclusively from ten dental directories. SOCi found ChatGPT recommended only 1.2% of the brand locations it studied, and those averaged 4.3 stars.

Listings and websites. Yext analyzed 6.8 million AI citations for location searches from July to August 2025. Across engines, websites supplied 44% and listings 42%, and OpenAI leaned on listings, at 48.7% [4]. Yext sells listings software, so read it as a vendor study. BrightLocal's test was small, 20 searches across 10 niches and four AI tools, and found Yelp used as a source in 33% of the searches overall [5].

Ratings. SOCi studied more than 350,000 locations across 2,751 multi-location brands and found ChatGPT recommended just 1.2% of them, against a 35.9% average appearance rate in Google's local 3-Pack [6]. The locations it did recommend averaged 4.3 stars [6].

Mentions. Ahrefs found YouTube mentions correlated most strongly with brand visibility across ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews, at about 0.737, with branded web mentions at 0.66 to 0.71 [7]. A 2025 research preprint found AI search engines showed "a systematic and overwhelming bias towards Earned media" [8]. In Whitespark's 2026 survey of local search experts, the top-rated AI visibility factor was presence on expert-curated "best of" lists, followed by a dedicated page for each service [9].

Steps: be findable, be consistent, be reviewed

Direct answer

Five steps are in your control. Allow OAI-SearchBot and check your CDN. Put clear answers about your services, prices and hours on your own pages. Make your business details identical across your site, Google Business Profile, Bing Places and the directories in your field. Earn genuine reviews, and reply to them. Earn mentions on the sites people in your industry trust.

  1. Be crawlable. Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt; allowing or blocking GPTBot is a separate decision. Ask your host or CDN to allow OpenAI's published crawler IPs.
  2. Answer on your own site. Give each service its own page with the facts customers ask about. Websites supplied 44% of the local AI citations Yext counted [4], and Whitespark's experts rank a dedicated page for each service near the top of their AI factors [9].
  3. Make listings match. OpenAI's help page points to Microsoft's privacy statement among its search providers, and Microsoft tells local businesses to register with Bing Places so their details stay eligible for AI answers [10]. Our reading: keep Bing Places, your Google Business Profile and the main directories in your field identical. See local citations.
  4. Earn reviews the right way. BrightLocal found 88% of AI users fact-check the reviews an AI cites [11], and Google's policies ban review incentives and review gating [12]. Ask every real customer. See do Google reviews help SEO.
  5. Earn mentions. Whitespark's experts also rate prominence on key industry sites and citations in news articles, blog posts, government sites and industry associations among the top AI factors [9].

What you can't control

Direct answer

No one can promise a ChatGPT recommendation. OpenAI says placement is not guaranteed, and its answers depend on how it rewrites the question and where the user is. AI search tools also make mistakes: in a Tow Center test on news excerpts, eight AI search tools collectively gave incorrect answers to more than 60 percent of queries. Check what ChatGPT says about you, and correct the sources behind it.

The Tow Center at Columbia Journalism Review tested the tools on news excerpts; Perplexity answered 37 percent of the queries incorrectly, and the researchers found that generative search tools fabricated links [13]. The lesson for a business is practical: if an assistant gets your hours or services wrong, the fix is usually in the sources it reads, your site and your listings, not in the assistant.

Anyone who claims a fixed ChatGPT ranking for your business is describing something OpenAI itself says it doesn't guarantee. Answers vary with the user's location and the query rewrite, so treat any single screenshot as one sample.

Measuring mentions across AI assistants

Direct answer

Measure ChatGPT with referral data first: OpenAI says ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to referral links, so those visits show in your analytics. Check server logs for OAI-SearchBot visits. Then sample the questions your customers ask, from your own location, on a schedule, and record whether you're named. Treat those samples as snapshots, not rankings.

For the other assistants, use their own tools: Bing Webmaster Tools reports how often your content is cited in generative answers [10], and Google's Search Console counts AI Overviews and AI Mode traffic in its Performance report [14]. Our guides to AI search optimization and how to rank in AI Overviews cover those in depth, and AEO vs GEO explains the vocabulary.

One more point, plainly: ChatGPT mentions don't count toward our first page guarantee. Its AI place is AI suggestions and answers as the written terms define them: the answer written by AI that Google shows on its results page, when it names or links your business, measured separately in each of the three places alongside the map results and page one of the organic results.

Frequently asked questions

Can you pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can promise you a recommendation. OpenAI's search help page says ChatGPT ranks results "using multiple factors intended to help users find relevant, reliable information," and that "Placement is not guaranteed." [2] Anyone promising a ChatGPT recommendation is promising more than OpenAI does. The durable route is the one in this article: be crawlable, consistent, reviewed and mentioned.

How does ChatGPT choose local businesses?

OpenAI says ChatGPT can use an approximate location based on your IP address and third-party providers for nearby business listings, without naming them [2]. Studies suggest listings carry weight: Yext found OpenAI's models leaned on listings for 48.7% of local citations [4]. SOCi found the locations ChatGPT recommended averaged 4.3 stars [6].

Does ChatGPT use Google reviews?

OpenAI doesn't say. Its help page mentions unnamed third-party providers for business listings, and names OpenTable, Resy and Yelp only for restaurant availability [2]. So don't assume your Google reviews feed ChatGPT; claims that they do are unsourced. Reviews still matter to the reader: BrightLocal found 88% of AI users fact-check the reviews an AI cites [11].

How long does it take to show up in ChatGPT?

OpenAI gives no timeline for being recommended. It does say a robots.txt change can take roughly 24 hours to be reflected for its crawlers [3], so unblocking OAI-SearchBot takes effect within about a day, though eligibility is not placement [2]. Being named also depends on the listings, reviews and mentions around your business, which take ongoing work.

Do ChatGPT mentions count toward the first page guarantee?

No. The AI place in our first page guarantee is AI suggestions and answers as the written terms define them: the answer written by AI that Google shows on its results page when it names or links your business. Mentions in ChatGPT, Perplexity or other assistants don't count toward the share, which is measured separately in each of the three places. The written guarantee terms have the details.

Is ChatGPT SEO different from Google SEO?

Less than it sounds. The foundations are shared: crawlable pages, clear answers, accurate listings and a good reputation. The differences are practical: ChatGPT needs OAI-SearchBot access, sometimes works through partner search providers, and tags referrals with utm_source=chatgpt.com [3][2][1]. Google, for its part, says optimizing for its own AI features is still SEO [15].
