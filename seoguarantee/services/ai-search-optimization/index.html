AI search (AIO / GEO)

AI SEO services with a guarantee in Google's AI answers

Our AI SEO services carry a guarantee for the AI answer Google shows on its results page: after an assessment, that answer will name or link your business for up to 50% of the key phrases where Google shows one, on a set schedule. It is the third place the first page guarantee measures, beside the map and organic results, and the work behind it is SEO, because Google says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO.

In AI answers the share is counted over the phrases where Google shows one, and AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini and AI Mode are not part of it. The problem: the answer sits above the results, and it names very few

For some searches, Google writes an answer before it lists a single website. Google launched AI Overviews to everyone in the U.S. on May 14, 2024, and Alphabet reported more than 2 billion monthly users by July 2025. When the answer is there, fewer people click: in Pew Research Center's March 2025 study of 900 U.S. adults, people who saw an AI summary clicked a regular result on 8% of visits, against 15% when there was none.

Being inside the answer is what pays. Seer Interactive's 2026 update found that brands cited in an AI Overview earned 120% more organic clicks per impression than when they were not cited, though still fewer than on searches with no AI Overview at all.

Local customers are asking AI as well. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 U.S. adults found that 45% had used AI tools for local business recommendations, up from 6% a year earlier.

The answers are uneven and selective. Google says AI Overviews "often don't trigger", and in our own check of 12 commercial local searches on September 27, 2026, Google showed one for just 2 of them. Where Google shows its AI local packs on mobile, Sterling Sky found they surface only about 32% as many businesses as the classic three-pack of map results. Few businesses get named, and the rest are left out of the answer.

AI SEO services in the plan

These are the AI search optimization services the plan carries. Each one is aimed at one number: the share of your phrases where Google's AI answer names or links you.

01

Pages worth citing

Google's advice for its AI features is to make "unique, non-commodity content", and it says a unique viewpoint that stands out can help. In the peer-reviewed GEO study (KDD 2024), adding citations, quotations and statistics raised visibility by 30-40% on one metric, a lab result on a test engine rather than a promise.

What you get: service pages and answers written from your own expertise, with the sources, figures and first-hand detail an AI answer can quote.

02

A site Google can index and quote

To be a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode, Google says a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and since 2026 the site must also not be opted out of these features in Search Console. Other AI tools read your site through their own crawlers: OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search, PerplexityBot for Perplexity, and Bing's index for Copilot. AI Mode and these tools do not count toward the first page guarantee.

What you get: a site that Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot can all crawl, with no setting that keeps your pages out of an answer by mistake.

03

A Business Profile and listings that agree

Google says its generative AI responses can include information about local businesses, and that Business Profiles can help your services show in AI responses. Microsoft points local businesses to Bing Places, and Google's Gemini app answers place questions from Google Maps data. The Gemini app does not count toward the first page guarantee.

What you get: your Google Business Profile, your Bing Places listing and your key directory listings saying the same thing as your website, handled with our local SEO work.

04

Mentions on sites the answers trust

In an Ahrefs study of 75K brands, web mentions correlated with AI Overview visibility at 0.664, against 0.218 for backlinks, a correlation rather than proof. Whitespark's experts put a business's presence on expert-curated "best of" lists first among AI visibility factors, and Google warns that inauthentic mentions are not as helpful as they might seem.

What you get: mentions of your business on relevant sites, industry associations and local publications.

05

AI answer tracking on each milestone

On each milestone date we search every phrase on your list from the market we agreed, record whether Google showed an AI answer, and whether it named or linked your business.

What you get: your AI share on each milestone date, counted over the phrases where Google showed an AI answer, next to the impressions Search Console's generative AI report shows for your site.

01 How we do it

How Google picks the sources for an AI answer

Google's documentation is clear that there is no separate system to game: "The best practices for SEO continue to be relevant because our generative AI features on Google Search are rooted in our core Search ranking and quality systems." Its AI features can run a "query fan-out", issuing several related searches across subtopics, which lets them link a wider and more varied set of pages than a classic results page.

That may be why the overlap with the organic top ten varies so much by study and by date. Ahrefs found 76% of cited pages ranked in the top ten in July 2025, but only about 38% after Gemini 3 in March 2026. BrightEdge put the share from the top ten at 16.7% in September 2025. No single figure is "the" answer, which is why we measure the pages cited for your own phrases instead of assuming.

Google also says what does not matter. "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." It needs no special schema and no AI text files, and on llms.txt it is blunt: "Doing so will neither harm nor help your site's visibility or rankings in Google Search, as Google Search ignores them." Its hiring advice even asks whether an AEO or GEO provider's advice follows Google's own guidance. AI Mode, one of the two features in that first quote, does not count toward the first page guarantee.

Business Profile data can end up in the answer itself. In one national check of "near me seo" on September 27, 2026, Google's AI Overview named two of the three businesses in the map results, with their star ratings and review counts, and linked neither. It is a single observation, but it shows why the profile and the website have to agree.

02 How we do it

What we change so you are the answer

Once the cited pages are measured, the work follows the gaps. We make sure every page you want quoted can be indexed and shown with a snippet. We rewrite or add the pages the answers draw on, with the facts, sources and plain answers the cited pages have, and the first-hand detail they lack. We line up your Business Profile, Bing Places and listings, and we build mentions on sites that already get cited for your phrases.

AI answers are not stable. Google says a page that meets every requirement may still not be indexed or served, and in SE Ranking's tests only 9.2% of AI Mode's cited URLs matched across three runs of the same searches. That is why the first page guarantee counts the AI answer on Google's results page, not AI Mode, on set dates, from your agreed market, and over the phrases where Google shows one.

03

Beyond Google: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini

Customers use other AI tools too. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found ChatGPT "used by 31% for business recommendations in the last 12 months", with Google's AI Mode at 23%. ChatGPT search launched on October 31, 2024 and sometimes uses outside search providers; Copilot runs on Bing's index; Gemini answers place questions from Google Maps.

The same work helps in each of them: pages worth citing, crawler access, listings that agree and mentions across the web. So we treat these tools as channels. OpenAI itself says placement in ChatGPT search "is not guaranteed", and none of these tools counts toward our first page guarantee: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini and AI Mode are not part of it, and only the AI answer Google shows on its results page is.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are AI SEO services?

AI SEO services are the work that gets a business named, linked or cited in AI-written answers. At First Page SEO Guarantee that means pages worth citing, a site AI crawlers can read, a Business Profile and listings that agree with your website, and mentions on trusted sites. Google says optimizing for its generative AI features is still SEO, so the work sits on top of ordinary SEO rather than replacing it.

What is the difference between AIO, GEO and AEO?

They are industry terms rather than Google's own. GEO, generative engine optimization, was coined in a peer-reviewed paper presented at KDD 2024. AEO means answer engine optimization. AIO is used both for Google's AI Overviews, a Google product, and for AI optimization in general. Google names AEO and GEO in its own guide and says that, from its point of view, optimizing for generative AI search is still SEO.

Does the first page guarantee include ChatGPT or Perplexity?

No. The AI place in the first page guarantee is the AI answer Google shows on its own results page, when it names or links your business, counted over the phrases where Google shows one. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini and Google's AI Mode are separate channels. The same SEO work helps in them, but they do not count toward the first page guarantee. OpenAI itself says placement in ChatGPT search is not assured.

Do I need an llms.txt file or special schema to appear in AI answers?

Not for Google. Google says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations for AI Overviews or AI Mode, no special schema to add, and that Google Search ignores llms.txt files, which neither help nor harm your visibility there. AI Mode itself does not count toward the first page guarantee. Structured data still helps Google understand a page and can earn rich results. What gets a page quoted is what gets it ranked: useful, original content on a site Google can index.

Why doesn't Google show an AI answer for every search?

Google says AI Overviews appear only when its systems judge them additive to classic Search, and that they "often don't trigger". Pew found an AI summary on 18% of the Google searches in its March 2025 study, more often for longer searches and for questions. In our own check of 12 commercial local searches in September 2026, 2 showed one. That is why the AI share is counted over the phrases where Google shows an AI answer.

How do you measure AI visibility?

On each milestone date we search every phrase on your list from your agreed market and record whether Google showed an AI answer and whether it named or linked your business. We add the impressions from Search Console's generative AI performance report, which reached all sites by August 31, 2026, and Bing Webmaster Tools' AI citation data. ChatGPT tags its referral visits with utm_source=chatgpt.com, so those show in your analytics too, though ChatGPT does not count toward the first page guarantee.
