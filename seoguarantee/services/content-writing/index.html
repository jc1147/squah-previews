The problem: pages that say what every competitor says

Many business websites say the same things in the same order, and Google has been pushing that kind of page down. Its helpful content system became part of its core ranking systems in March 2024, and after those changes Google reported 45% less low-quality, unoriginal content in its results, by its own count. Its March 2024 spam policy update treats pages mass-produced to manipulate rankings as abuse, "no matter whether content is produced through automation, human efforts, or some combination of human and automated processes."

The upside is just as plain. Google's starter guide says content people find compelling and useful "will likely influence your website's presence in search results more than any of the other suggestions in this guide." For topics that affect people's health, finances or safety, where many practices sit, Google says its systems give even more weight to content that shows experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust.

AI answers raise the bar again. Google tells site owners to focus on "unique, non-commodity content", and says a unique viewpoint that stands out can help. A page that repeats the other ten has nothing for an answer to quote.

01 How we do it

How we plan content from page one

The plan is built from measurement, the same way our SEO is:

  • Start from the results page. Google's layout for a phrase shows what searchers want: a guide, a service page, a list or a map. Sistrix found that search intent sets the layout, and the layout sets how many organic clicks exist. We write the format the phrase calls for.
  • List what the ranking pages cover. Google says relevance starts with matching words, then looks for related content beyond the keyword. We record the topics, questions and examples each page-one result covers.
  • Find the gaps. Semrush sorts keywords into groups such as "Missing" (your competitors rank and you do not) and "Weak" (you rank below all of them). Those groups, per phrase, are the writing plan.
  • Cover it, then go past it. Google's helpful-content questions ask whether a page adds substantial value and originality instead of rewriting its sources. So "everything they did and more" means covering what page one covers and adding what it lacks: first-hand detail, a clear answer, a real example.

There is no length target. Google says there is no magical word count, minimum or maximum, so a page is as long as its topic needs.

02 How we do it

How we write so people and AI answers use it

A page has two readers now: the person searching and the AI answer summarizing the results.

  • Answer first. The main point sits at the top, in a sentence that can stand on its own.
  • Show the evidence. In the peer-reviewed GEO study (KDD 2024), adding citations, quotations and statistics raised visibility in AI answers by 30-40% on one metric, a lab result rather than a promise.
  • Structure for scanning. Microsoft says assistants can often lift question-and-answer pairs word for word into their answers. Google says there is no need to break content into tiny pieces for its AI. We use clear headings and FAQ blocks.
  • Put a name on it. Google strongly encourages accurate bylines where readers expect them, and trust is the part of E-E-A-T it calls most important.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are SEO content writing services?

SEO content writing services plan, write and edit the text a website needs to rank and to persuade: service pages, area pages, blog posts and supporting copy. At First Page SEO Guarantee each piece is planned from the pages already on page one for its phrase, written by our writers and editors, and linked into the rest of the site, as part of the plan behind the first page guarantee.

What types of content do you write?

Page text for your services and the areas you serve, blog posts and guides that answer the questions customers ask, and press releases when your business has real news. We also rewrite pages you already have so they cover what the page-one results cover. Every piece is written by the same team that runs your SEO, website and conversion work, so the words and the rankings plan agree.

How long should a page or blog post be?

As long as the topic needs, and no longer. Google says there is no magical word count target, minimum or maximum, and that it does not use the keywords meta tag at all. We look at what the page-one results cover for the phrase, cover the same ground, add what they lack, and stop. Padding a page to hit a number helps neither the reader nor the ranking.

How does content help with Google's AI answers?

Google says its AI features are rooted in its core ranking and quality systems, so the pages that rank well are among the ones those answers draw on. Google also asks for unique, non-commodity content and says there is no need to write in a special way for AI. In the peer-reviewed GEO study, adding citations, quotations and statistics made pages more visible in AI answers on one metric.

How does content connect to the first page guarantee?

Content is part of the plan behind the first page guarantee. It is how we earn the guaranteed rankings: the pages that win phrases in the organic results, feed Google's AI answers and support your Business Profile in the map results. The first page guarantee itself is about rankings: after an assessment, we guarantee up to 50% of the key phrases you could rank for will rank on the first page of Google.

Can you write for dental and medical practices?

Yes, and health topics get extra scrutiny. Google gives more weight to strong experience, expertise and trust on topics that can affect people's health, and professional codes add limits: the ADA Code asks dentists to keep websites and SEO truthful, the ASPS Code bars misleading before-and-after photos, and the APA Code bars psychologists from soliciting testimonials from current therapy clients. Your own counsel or board has the final word.
