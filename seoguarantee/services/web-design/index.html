The problem: a website that looks nice and does nothing

In our experience, a successful website is not about looking nice. It is about turning visitors into customers, and before that, about being found at all.

Most pages never are. Ahrefs found that 96.55% of the pages in its index get no traffic from Google, and in Advanced Web Ranking's July 2026 data the first three organic results took roughly 92% of the clicks that reached the top ten. A site that is not built to rank splits what is left.

When people do arrive, the site has to earn the next step. In Tebra's 2025 survey of 3,964 U.S. adults, 44% said a practice's website influences their choice of provider. If your website has traffic and no customers, it needs an upgrade.

Some businesses never had a real site at all. Google turned off the websites made with Business Profiles in March 2024, and their redirects ran only until June 10, 2024.

01 How we do it

How we build a site to rank

Every build starts from the measurement, not from a theme:

  • The structure comes from the phrase list. One page per service, and for multi-city businesses, each city gets its own page with its own content, written to stand on its own rather than as a copy.
  • The words match the searches. Titles and main headings use the words people search with, as Google advises.
  • Links connect the pages. Google uses links to find pages and to judge how relevant they are, so every page is linked from the pages that lead to it.
  • Markup matches the page. Structured data helps Google understand a page and can earn rich results. Google does not call it a ranking boost.
  • Your details are crawlable. In Whitespark's survey, a name, address and phone on your site that match your Business Profile is a map factor, and a site with no crawlable name, address and phone is on the list of negative factors.
  • Speed is measured. Core Web Vitals are checked against Google's thresholds before launch, and again after.

The same pages are what Google's AI answers can draw on. Google says its generative AI features are rooted in its core ranking systems, and that a page must be indexed and able to show a snippet to be a supporting link. A site built this way serves all three places the first page guarantee measures.

02 How we do it

How we build a site to convert

A ranking only matters if the visit becomes a customer. That is why the team that builds your site also runs your conversion work: we find where users leave the site, ask why, and test changes.

  • One clear next step per page. Every page says what to do next and makes it easy.
  • Navigation built for real use. Top and bottom toolbars, custom dynamic menus and multi-menus, so people reach the page they came for.
  • Forms wired to your systems. Website-to-CRM, website-to-email and POS connections mean a lead or an order is not retyped by hand.
  • Portals for the customers you have. Client, education and recruitment portals give people a place to log in, learn or apply.

Conversion rate percentages on this page would be guesses, so there are none. We measure yours instead, and our content writers write the words each page needs.
