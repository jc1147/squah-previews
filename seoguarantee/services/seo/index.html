The problem: page two is where customers stop looking

A customer who needs you types a few words into Google and picks from what the first page shows. The clicks gather at the top. In Advanced Web Ranking's study of US desktop results for July 2026, the first organic result had an average click-through rate of 20.02%, the second 10.36% and the third 3.89%, and the first three together took roughly 92% of the clicks that reached the top ten.

Past page one, the clicks nearly stop. In Backlinko's data (revised 2022), only 0.63% of Google searchers clicked anything on the second page. Sistrix measured under 1% for every result on page two.

Page one does not arrive on its own, either. Ahrefs followed a million random new pages and found that only 1.74% reached the top ten within a year. And the page is crowded: Advanced Web Ranking found AI Overviews, the AI answers Google shows above the results, on 43% of sampled desktop results pages in July 2026, and where one appeared, the first organic result's click rate fell by 52%.

Nobody can buy their way into these results. Google says it "never accepts money to include or rank sites in our search results". Page one is earned, page by page and phrase by phrase, and a business that is not there is handing those customers to the competitors who are.

In the plan

SEO services in the plan

These are the SEO services the plan carries. Each one is aimed at the same number: your share of the phrase list on page one.

01

Keyword strategy and your phrase list

We build a list of every variation your customers could type to find a business like yours, and we go after all of them. The list comes from your services, the words people add to them ("near me", "cost", "emergency", "open now") and the places you serve, checked against Google's own search data, which is rounded and set by location. Searches for your own business name do not count.

What you get: a written phrase list, agreed with you at the start, that the first page guarantee is measured against.

02

On-page SEO

Google's advice is plain: use the words people search with, and put them in prominent places such as the title and the main heading. We give every service you sell a page of its own, because in Whitespark's 2026 survey of 47 local search experts, a dedicated page for each service led the list of local organic ranking factors.

What you get: on page SEO services across every page that should rank, from titles, headings and internal links to structured data that matches what the page says.

03

Technical SEO

Google Search works in three stages, crawling, indexing and serving results, and not every page makes it through all three. Technical SEO keeps your pages moving through them: a clean site structure, working redirects, templates measured for speed and no pages blocked by mistake. Google uses Core Web Vitals in ranking and rates a page good when its main content loads within 2.5 seconds, it responds to input in under 200 milliseconds and its layout shift stays under 0.1.

What you get: technical SEO services that keep Google able to crawl, index and serve your pages, with speed measured against those thresholds.

04

Content that covers what page one covers

Google's starter guide says content people find compelling and useful will likely influence your presence in search more than any of its other suggestions. So for each phrase we list the topics the page-one pages cover, find the ones your page misses, and add what they lack: first-hand detail, clear answers and your own photos.

What you get: new and rewritten pages that match the depth of page one and go past it, written by our writers and editors.

05

Link building

Google uses links to find pages and to judge how relevant they are, and it says PageRank is still part of its core ranking systems. Ahrefs' rule of thumb for the top ten is to acquire as many links as the pages already there, if not more, which is our method in one line.

What you get: a list of the sites that link to the pages ahead of you and not to yours, worked down with links from relevant businesses, local organizations and publications.

06

A report on each milestone

On each milestone date we search every phrase on your list from the market we agreed, record where your business shows up in each of the three places, and send you the results. You can ask for the same report at any time between milestones, and we run it within ten days.

What you get: your share in the map results, the organic results and the AI answers, counted phrase by phrase and never blended into one score.

01 How we do it

How we measure the gap

This is the method. It is arithmetic over measurements, not a promise of any one ranking, and Google still decides the outcome.

For each phrase on your list we search Google from your agreed market, because Google says its results depend on the searcher's location and settings. We record what shows: the map results, the ten organic results and, when Google shows one, the AI answer. Then we measure the businesses and pages already there:

  • Content: the topics, depth and format of each ranking page, against yours.
  • On-page: titles, headings, internal links and structured data.
  • Links: how many sites link to each ranking page, an input Ahrefs and Semrush build their keyword difficulty scores from.
  • Business Profile: the categories, the review count, rating and pace of new reviews, the photos and the citations of the businesses in the map results.
  • AI answer: whether one appears, and which pages and businesses it names or links.

Every one of those inputs comes from outside Google. Google says third-party tools have no access to its internal ranking data, and Ahrefs, which sells one of those tools, says no difficulty metric can accurately predict your chances of ranking. So we treat each score as an estimate and study the pages behind it.

The gap is the distance between you and the weakest business still holding a page-one slot in each place. That is the business you have to pass first, not the leader.

02 How we do it

How we close it

Once the gaps are measured, the plan is an order of work. Phrases with the smallest gaps go first, then the harder ones, so your share of page one builds in waves toward each milestone. The schedule in the written terms is at least 20% of your list on the first page by day 90, 40% by day 180 and 50%+ by the end of the first year, with each share measured separately in each of the three places.

We win phrases with useful pages and strong links. Google makes broad core updates several times a year; the written terms say those changes do not excuse a missed milestone, and keeping up with them is part of the work.

What we will not promise is a position. Google's own advice on hiring an SEO is blunt:

We agree. The first page guarantee names no position and is never every phrase on your list. It is a share of the list on page one, by set dates, in each place.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do small business SEO services include?

At First Page SEO Guarantee, they include a keyword strategy built on an agreed phrase list, on-page and technical SEO, content that covers what the page-one results cover, links from relevant sites, and a report on each milestone date. Everything is measured against the businesses already on page one for each phrase. SEO sits inside the Full Suite, so web design, content writing, local SEO, AI search optimization and conversion work come from the same team.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Google's current guidance says some changes take effect in a few hours and others take several months, and suggests waiting a few weeks before judging. Ahrefs found that 40.82% of the pages that reached the top ten did so within a month. Our written terms set the milestones: at least 20% of your phrase list on the first page by day 90, 40% by day 180 and 50%+ by the end of the first year, in each of the three places separately.

Can an SEO company guarantee first page rankings?

Not for every phrase, and never for a particular position: Google's own hiring advice says no one can promise the top ranking. Our guaranteed small business SEO is narrower and written down. After an assessment, we guarantee up to 50% of the key phrases you could rank for will rank on the first page of Google, by set dates, measured separately in each of the three places. The written terms set out how each share is checked.

What is technical SEO, and is it part of the plan?

Technical SEO is the work that lets Google crawl, index and show your pages: site structure, redirects, speed, mobile layout and markup. It is part of the SEO work in the plan, and because the same team builds and maintains websites, the fixes ship on your site without a hand-off to another vendor. Google uses Core Web Vitals in ranking, but it says good scores alone do not decide who ranks at the top, so speed work always sits beside content and links.

Do I still need SEO if I run paid ads?

Paid ads and organic results are separate. Google says it never accepts money to include or rank sites in its organic results, so an ad budget buys clicks only while it runs. Organic rankings are earned, and they do not switch off when a budget does, though Google says no position is fixed in place. One client, Liquor License Agents, moved from heavy spending on paid advertising to our SEO service, as its case page describes.

How do you measure SEO results?

We count phrases. On each milestone date we search every phrase on your agreed list from your market and record whether your business shows up in the map results, the organic results and Google's AI answer. Your share in each place is the phrases that count there divided by the phrases on the list; in AI answers it is counted over the phrases for which Google shows one. It is a plain count, not a click-weighted share of voice.
