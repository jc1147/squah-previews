From the Valley to the high country

Where Arizona's customers are, and how we reach each market

Your customers type their own city into the search, so Gilbert and Tucson get different lists. Name your service area in Step 1 and we map out the pages it needs.

Most Arizonans live in the Valley, where Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale and Scottsdale run into one another. Can you help a business outside the Valley, in Tucson or Flagstaff?

Anywhere in the state. Your pages follow your customers, whether they live in Yuma, Prescott Valley or Sierra Vista, and each one talks to that town rather than to Arizona in general.

How does SEO fit into the rest of my digital marketing?

Digital marketing covers everything that wins you customers online; SEO is the piece that puts you in front of someone already looking for your service. Our guaranteed marketing is our first page guarantee: it concerns where you rank and never promises sales. Business Profile care and review requests come with it, while website design and conversion work belongs to our Full Suite.

Should my business have a page for every Valley city?

Only for the cities that bring you work. Scottsdale, Peoria and Goodyear customers each search under their own city name, so a single Phoenix page rarely wins them. We begin with the places that pay best and add others as you grow.

We serve customers in Phoenix and in Tucson. How does that work?

Each city gets pages of its own, and each location its own Business Profile and reviews. Your name, address and hours stay word for word the same on every listing, so Google can tell your locations apart and trust both.

Do winter visitors change how an Arizona business should market itself?

For many trades they do. Part-time residents arrive in the cooler months and search for the same services everyone else needs, often by neighborhood or community name, so we have your seasonal pages, hours and service area ready before they come back each year.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • Share of Arizonans living in Maricopa County † is derived: Maricopa County's July 2025 residents divided by Arizona's.

  • People who moved to Arizona in a typical year † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Change in Arizona's residents since April 2020 † is derived: Arizona's July 2025 residents minus the April 2020 starting estimate.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.
