What Phoenix owners want to know before hiring an SEO company

Which parts of Phoenix and the Valley can you cover?

All of it, one area at a time. Customers in Laveen, Arcadia and Deer Valley search differently, and so do people in Mesa or Glendale, so we start with the parts of town that bring you the best work and add pages as you grow. A plumber based near Paradise Valley and one based in Laveen rarely want the same first page, and your plan reflects where your own jobs come from.

Does a Phoenix business need SEO, digital marketing or both?

Both, and one sits inside the other: SEO is the part of your digital marketing that meets people at the moment they search. Our first page guarantee is a promise about rankings, never about sales, and with it come Business Profile work, review requests and, through our Full Suite if you want them, website design and conversion.

Have you worked with businesses in my line of work?

Probably. The businesses on our list, all on page one of Google, work in very different trades, including a law firm found in Phoenix searches and three med spas and two dental practices elsewhere in the country. The method is the same for each: find the searches that bring real customers, then build the pages that answer them. Once we have assessed your business, you will know which of your searches we can realistically take on.

Why pay for SEO when I could just run ads?

Ads stop the moment the budget does. A page that ranks keeps working while it holds its place, and you pay no fee for each click. Plenty of businesses run both; our first page guarantee is agreed after an assessment, so you know what the SEO side is working toward.

What will you need from me once we start?

Access to your website and Business Profile, a list of the services you most want to sell and the parts of town you serve, and a few minutes now and then to approve new pages. We handle the writing, the listings and the reporting.

Should my Business Profile list every service I offer?

Yes, within reason. Google reads the services, categories and questions on your profile when it decides whom to show on the map, so we fill them in carefully, keep them in line with your website and add photos from real jobs around Phoenix.

Will I need a new website first?

Rarely. We start from your current site and repair what customers trip over first, such as slow pages, a quote form that breaks on a phone or opening hours that differ from your profile. If the site itself is the problem, the assessment will say so.

Where our figures come from

Data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau: Population Estimates, Vintage 2025 (July 1, 2025); American Community Survey, 2020-2024 5-year estimates; 2022 Economic Census.

How the figures marked † were derived

  • People who moved to Phoenix in a typical year (at least) † is derived: residents one year and over who lived in another county, another state or abroad a year earlier, added together; moves from elsewhere in Maricopa County are not in the survey table, so it is a floor; a typical year of the 2020-2024 surveys.

  • Change in Phoenix's residents since April 2020 † is derived: the city's July 2025 residents minus the April 2020 starting estimate.

  • Phoenix residents who work in their own business † is derived: employed residents 16 and over who work in their own business, incorporated or not, added together.

  • Share of Maricopa County living in Phoenix † is derived: the county's July 2025 residents inside the Phoenix city limits divided by all its residents.

  • Share who speak Spanish at home † is derived: residents 5 and over who speak Spanish at home divided by all residents 5 and over.
