Who is doing this

A desk with instruments
and no clients to protect.

I'm James. I run this desk, and I don't sell anything to the people I measure. I got tired of watching small businesses pay for "visibility" nobody ever verified — when the report and the invoice come from the same desk, that's not reporting, that's marketing.

So this desk does the checking. Geo-grid scans of every client on an agency's roster — not the three they show off. Speed tests on every site they built. Template forensics on every "custom" design. If an agency's results are good, the file will say so. So far, the files say something else.

Nobody pays to be excluded and nobody pays to be included. That's the whole business model: there isn't one on this page.

Roster discovery

We find every client an agency serves — showcase pages, template fingerprints, the code they leave behind in page source. The whole book, not the highlight reel.

Geo-grid scans

Each business is scanned across a grid of real map points around its own location, and its rank is recorded at every pin. 116,765 points so far.

The same tests on every site: PageSpeed, template reuse, on-page basics. Instrument readings, not opinions.
