The privilege

What SF-2003 lets you do — and what it does not

Type 21 privileges and limits in San Francisco. These are class rules and they apply to SF-2003 like every other Type 21.
ClassType 21 — Off-Sale General
In plain EnglishA liquor store. Beer, wine and distilled spirits sold in sealed containers, taken away and drunk somewhere else.
Food obligationNone. There is no eating-place requirement on an off-sale licence.
MinorsMay be on the premises and may work there subject to the usual rules, but may not purchase.
HoursCalifornia permits sale from 6:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. San Francisco off-sale licences commonly carry conditions narrowing that, and those conditions transfer with the licence.
Quota statusQuota class. San Francisco is over the county ceiling for off-sale general licences, so no new original issues and the only supply is an existing holder.
Moving it to another addressA premises-to-premises transfer inside San Francisco County is possible with ABC approval and a fresh 30-day posting. Moving a quota licence out of the county is generally not available.
Local approvalSeparate from ABC, and the harder half. Parts of San Francisco restrict new off-sale alcohol uses under the Planning Code, and a conditional use may be required at your address.

Process

How long SF-2003 takes to put in your name

Median day counts from our 64 closed California files. They add to 78 days from accepted offer to a licence issued in your name, and 30 of those are a statutory posting period nobody can shorten.

  1. Offer accepted, escrow opened7 days

    Purchase agreement, then escrow with a licensed escrow holder. This is the point your money moves, not completion day.

  2. Buyer qualification and application10 days

    Person-to-person transfer application, Live Scan for every principal, proof of funds. The mutual NDA is signed here and the confidential file on SF-2003 is released to you.

  3. Public notice posted at the premises30 days

    ABC posts the application at the premises and the notice of intended transfer is published. Statutory, and it cannot be shortened whatever anyone promises you.

  4. Protest period and local agency review14 days

    Any protest filed during the posting is worked through here. A Planning conditional use, if your district needs one, runs on its own much longer calendar.

  5. ABC investigation and approval10 days

    The investigator confirms the premises, the qualification of the buyer and the tax position of the seller.

  6. Escrow closes, licence issued in your name7 days

    Approval issued, escrow released to the seller, SF-2003 recorded against you. You can trade.

Stage day counts are medians from our own closed California files and add to 7 + 10 + 30 + 14 + 10 + 7 = 78 days. The 30-day posting is statutory; the rest is how San Francisco files actually run.
