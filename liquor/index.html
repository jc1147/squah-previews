Needs Clint before this goes live

None of these are claimed on the pages any more, so nothing on there is wrong while they are pending. They are blanks, not errors.

  • A form endpoint. There is no backend. The form composes a real email with the answers filled in, to an address on the client's own domain that receives mail. A real endpoint replaces that.
  • The real commission, if he wants one published at all.
  • A real transfer timeline, from his own closed files.
  • Whether Type 20 is quota-limited in San Francisco. The page no longer claims either way. It needs his ABC contact, not a guess.
  • Whether to name anyone as the contact, and who.
