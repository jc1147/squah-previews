Outreach plan, updated

October: 16 emails and a text every day

  • Mondays: a week-overview email (the whole week, events called out) + a text.
  • Tuesday-Sunday: a text every day with that day's deals; emails on the themed days in the table.
  • Event deals (Dime Days, Halloween when confirmed) lead the overview of their week and get their own email + text on the day.
  • Texts go out as MMS with the deal's 1024 'Text x Email' graphic from the sheet's formats.
