The three simulations

2. VIX futures timing rule

⚠ Works on paper, not at $10k

The idea: sell a VIX futures contract when it is priced above a fixed forecast of where volatility should go; otherwise sit in cash. Published by Cheng (2019).

  • The rule makes most of its money when the VIX contract is priced above 20, and that is exactly when a $10k account cannot afford even one micro contract. So at $10k it sits out the good days and pays costs on the rest.
  • Even on paper the edge is not statistically strong enough (t 1.48 against 2.0), so it would only ever be a small, closely watched position.
  • It reproduced the published research exactly, and it starts to work at about $25k.

3. Kalshi maker bids (research only)

✗ Both versions fail

The idea: post buy orders on the favourite side of Kalshi's daily bitcoin/ether price contracts and daily temperature contracts, and collect the small edge that favourites have historically had.

Realistic fills (only trades that actually reached your price)Best case: every order filled instantly
Crypto contracts: cumulative profit at $100 per event
Temperature contracts: cumulative profit at $100 per event
  • Crypto: the lines look good for Jul 2025 to 4 Aug 2026, but in the earlier test period every crypto version lost money (the chosen one: -0.98% per event). After allowing for the 12 versions tried, the result is not strong enough (t 2.36 against 2.9).
  • Temperature: it passes the statistics, but realistic fills earn +1.40% per event against +3.25% in the best case. You mostly get filled when the price is about to move against you, and that gap failed our check.
  • Kalshi also carries Nevada legal risk: the state Gaming Control Board says Kalshi's Nevada operations are unlawful. Nothing was traded.

The decision

The rule we wrote before testing says: if the closed-end fund strategy and the Kalshi strategy both fail, stop building active strategies. Both failed. Two ways to go from here:

A. Follow the stop rule

Stop active-strategy work for now. Two free monitors (not built yet) would watch for conditions that reopen a trade: the CME bitcoin futures premium, and index option selling regaining an edge.

B. Also keep the VIX rule on paper

It costs nothing and builds real evidence for when the account reaches about $25k. It needs:

  • two small fixes to the daily script;
  • one run each trading day after 4:15 pm ET (a scheduled task, or by hand).

What could reopen active trading: more capital (the VIX rule works from about $25k), legal clarity on Kalshi in Nevada, or running your buddy's framework as a paper-only practice desk. This is research, not financial advice.
