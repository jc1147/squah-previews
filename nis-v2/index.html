Who is doing this

A desk with instruments
and skin in the game.

I'm James. I own marketing agencies, and I built SGEN, the software stack they run on. The agencies in these files are my competitors. That is not a confession; it is the qualification. A critic has opinions. A competitor has instruments, benchmarks, and every reason to be exact, because the people being measured would love nothing more than to catch this desk wrong.

I'm done watching shops in my industry bill small businesses every month for visibility the map says does not exist. So this desk measures an agency's entire client book, not the three logos on the homepage: geo-grid scans of every listing, speed tests on every site, template forensics on every “custom” design. If an agency's results are good, the file says so. So far, the files say something else.

Nobody pays to be excluded and nobody pays to be included. That part has not changed and never will. The rest of the model is printed on the door: the files are free, your own listing is free, and if what you find in yours makes you want it fixed, the free full audit is one click away. That is the whole model, in daylight: the receipts cost nothing, and the offer at the end of them has a name on it.

Methodology · Chain of custody

How a business becomes
a data point.

Method sheet M-01Published with every dossierEvery figure recomputable
01

Roster discovery

We find every client an agency serves: showcase pages, template fingerprints, the code they leave behind in page source. The whole book, not the highlight reel.

02

Map checks

For every business, its main phrase is searched from a grid of real spots around its address, the way customers on phones do, and where Google put it is written down each time. 131,285 searches so far.

03

Website checks

The same tests on every site: how long it takes to show on a phone, whether it is a copy of a shared design, and whether the basics Google needs are filled in. Instrument readings, not opinions.

04

Published

Every figure lands in a public dossier and can be recomputed from the steps above.
