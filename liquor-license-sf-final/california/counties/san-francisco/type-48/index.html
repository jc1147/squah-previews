A Type 48 is an on-sale general licence for public premises: a bar, a tavern, a nightclub. It permits beer, wine and distilled spirits for consumption on the premises, exactly as a Type 47 does, and it carries no obligation to run a kitchen or serve meals. For an operator building a drinks-led room, that is the point of it.

The trade-off is absolute and it is the first thing to understand about the class: persons under 21 may not enter and remain on a Type 48 premises. Not with a parent, not for a meal, not for an all-ages show, not at four in the afternoon. That closes off families, most daytime food trade, and a large part of the live-music economy. Operators who discover this after buying are the unhappiest people in this market.

There is one Type 48 on our San Francisco book, asking $260,000. That is not an accident of our inventory: Type 48 licences change hands rarely in this city. The rooms that hold them tend to be long-established, and when one does come free it is usually sold with the business rather than as a licence on its own.

Because the supply is one deep, the honest advice on this class is to tell us early rather than to watch the page. If a Type 48 is genuinely what your concept needs, we will work the off-market book and the operators we know rather than wait for a listing. If a Type 47 with a real kitchen would also work, it is a deeper market, a faster search and usually a better price.

Process

Transferring a Type 48 in San Francisco

Day counts are medians from our 64 closed California files and add to 78 days, which is also the median from accepted offer to a licence issued in your name. Thirty of them are a statutory posting period nobody can shorten. A person-to-person transfer, where you take the licence at its existing address, is the quickest route; a premises-to-premises transfer adds a fresh posting at the new address and, in many districts, a Planning conditional use on its own calendar.

  1. Offer accepted, escrow opened7 days

    A purchase agreement, then escrow with a licensed escrow holder. California requires the full consideration to sit in escrow before the Department will approve the transfer, so this is the point your money moves, not completion day.

  2. Buyer qualification and application10 days

    The person-to-person transfer application, Live Scan fingerprinting for every principal, and proof of funds. This is also when the mutual NDA is signed and the confidential file — licence number, licensee of record, premises address, term, encumbrances, tax position — is released to you.

  3. Public notice posted at the premises30 days

    ABC posts the application at the premises for 30 days, and the notice of intended transfer is published in a newspaper of general circulation. The 30 days are statutory and cannot be shortened, whatever anyone promises you.

  4. Protest period and local agency review14 days

    Any protest filed during the posting is worked through here, and this is where San Francisco can add time: the Police Department’s alcohol liaison reviews on-sale applications, and a Planning conditional use, if your district needs one, runs on its own much longer calendar.

  5. ABC investigation and approval10 days

    The investigator confirms the premises, the qualification of the buyer and the tax position of the seller. Clean files clear here without anyone hearing from anyone.

  6. Escrow closes, licence issued in your name7 days

    Approval issued, escrow released to the seller, licence recorded against you. You can trade.

Stage day counts are the medians from our own closed California files and add to 7 + 10 + 30 + 14 + 10 + 7 = 78 days, which is also our median from accepted offer to issue. The 30-day posting is a statutory period; the rest is how San Francisco files actually run.

Questions

Type 48 questions we are actually asked

Can under-21s ever come into a Type 48 premises?

Not to enter and remain. This is the defining limit of the class and it is a rule, not a door policy: no accompanied minors, no all-ages shows, no children with a parent at four in the afternoon. If any part of your concept needs people under 21 in the room — food service, daytime trade, all-ages music — the licence you want is a Type 47 with a real kitchen, not a Type 48.

What is the difference between a Type 48 and a Type 47?

Same drinks, different obligations. A Type 47 must operate as a bona fide eating place with a working kitchen, and minors are permitted. A Type 48 has no food obligation at all, and nobody under 21 may enter and remain. Neither is an upgrade of the other. Pick on the room you intend to run.

Do I need an entertainment permit as well?

If the room puts on live music, DJs or extended-hours events, yes — that is a San Francisco Entertainment Commission permit, and it is a separate application on a separate calendar from the ABC transfer and from any Planning conditional use. Three approvals, three clocks. Start the entertainment one early; it is the one most often forgotten until the room is built.

Why is there only one on the book?

Because that is what the San Francisco market for this class looks like. Type 48s here tend to sit in long-established rooms and change hands with the business rather than on their own. If a Type 48 is what your concept needs, tell us now rather than watching this page — we work the off-market book and the operators we know, and that is how this class usually moves.

Can I convert a Type 48 into a Type 47 later?

Not by converting the licence. You would be buying a Type 47 on the open market at whatever it costs then, and dealing separately with what happens to the Type 48 you hold. Deciding between them now, before either is bought, is enormously cheaper than deciding later.
