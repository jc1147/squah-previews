A Type 44 licence issued by the Town of Morristown, in Morris County. Licence only: no premises, no fixtures, no goodwill. Type 44 is its own class, cited at N.J.S.A. 33:1-12(4), and it is not a consumption licence — do not read the Type 33 wording on our other pages across to this one.

An honest gap, stated where you will actually see it. Our rule set gives us the statutory citation for Type 44 and nothing else: not the statutory title, not the privilege wording. “Retail distribution” is our shorthand for the class, supplied by us and not quoted from the statute, and we have deliberately written no privilege statement rather than invent one. Read N.J.S.A. 33:1-12(4) yourself, or ask us and we will get the title and the privilege from ABC counsel in writing before you sign anything.

Asked at $415,000, inside a $385,000–$445,000 band set on 6 September 2026. Morris is the second dearest county median in New Jersey at a measured $415,000 — the same figure as this ask, which is where the price came from. Be careful with that comparison: the census median is weighted by consumption licences and this is not one. Of our 14 closed Morris files only 3 were Type 44, which is far too few to quote a median from; the table below shows the two most recent, at $372,000 and $348,000.

Fewer Type 44s change hands than Type 33s, so pricing is thinner, more town-specific, and the clerk's office knows the form less well. Morristown hears transfers on the second and fourth Tuesday and the next filing deadline is 13 October 2026; nothing has been refused in Morristown since 2019, but Type 44 transfers are rare enough there that the clerk asks for the file early. Suited to a package or bottle-shop operation rather than a bar — and if a bar is what you want, the Type 33 and Type 32 licences on this book are the classes to look at, not this one.

Process

What happens next, and how long each part takes

Day counts are medians from our own 168 closed files. They add up to 94 days, which is also the median time from accepted offer to a licence issued in the buyer's name.

  1. Offer accepted, contract signed7 days

    Deposit of 10% into escrow — $41,500 at this asking price. Contract is conditional on municipal approval; if the town refuses, the deposit comes back in full.

  2. Buyer qualification14 days

    Proof of funds and a completed ABC personal history. This is also the point the mutual NDA is signed and the full confidential file is released to you.

  3. Application filed21 days

    ABC-Form-12A to the municipality, with the transfer papers and the tax clearance request. Clearance itself typically takes 21 days and runs in parallel.

  4. Public notice published14 days

    Notice in the newspaper of record under N.J.A.C. 13:2-7.1. This is the window in which objections can be filed.

  5. Municipal hearing30 days

    Morristown's governing body hears transfers on the second and fourth Tuesday. The next filing deadline is 13 October 2026. Your counsel attends; you usually do too.

  6. Licence issued in your name8 days

    Resolution adopted, state paperwork completed, escrow released to the seller. You can trade.
