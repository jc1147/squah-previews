Process

What happens next, and how long each part takes

Day counts are medians from our own 168 closed files. They add up to 94 days, which is also the median time from accepted offer to a licence issued in the buyer's name.

  1. Offer accepted, contract signed7 days

    Deposit of 10% into escrow — $11,500 at this asking price. Contract is conditional on municipal approval; if the town refuses, the deposit comes back in full.

  2. Buyer qualification14 days

    Proof of funds and a completed ABC personal history. This is also the point the mutual NDA is signed and the full confidential file is released to you.

  3. Application filed21 days

    ABC-Form-12A to the municipality, with the transfer papers and the tax clearance request. Clearance itself typically takes 21 days and runs in parallel.

  4. Public notice published14 days

    Notice in the newspaper of record under N.J.A.C. 13:2-7.1. This is the window in which objections can be filed.

  5. Municipal hearing30 days

    Atlantic City's governing body hears transfers on the second and fourth Wednesday. The next filing deadline is 8 October 2026. Your counsel attends; you usually do too.

  6. Licence issued in your name8 days

    Resolution adopted, state paperwork completed, escrow released to the seller. You can trade.
