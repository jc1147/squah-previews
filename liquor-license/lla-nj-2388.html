A Type 33 plenary retail consumption licence issued by the Borough of Paramus, in Bergen County. Licence only: no premises, no fixtures, no goodwill. The privilege is on-premise consumption of beer, wine and distilled spirits, with packaged sales confined to the principal public barroom (N.J.S.A. 33:1-12(1)).

Paramus is most of why this one asks what it asks. The borough is retail before it is anything else — two regional malls and Route 4 and Route 17 running straight through it — and it sits at its cap under N.J.S.A. 33:1-12.14, one consumption licence per 3,000 residents. Nothing new gets issued. The licence moves place-to-place anyplace inside Paramus on the governing body's approval, and it will not cross the borough line unless both towns consent and the receiving town is under its own cap.

Asked at $475,000, inside a $440,000–$500,000 band set on 2 August 2026 and unchanged since. That is $105,000 above the $370,000 median ask across the 26 Bergen licences listed publicly, and it is the top of our Bergen book rather than the middle of it. Our own closed file — 31 Bergen transfers between 2019 and 2026 — settled at a $341,000 median, 7.8% below the asking median.

Suited to an operator who wants to be inside the Route 17 corridor and has decided the traffic is worth the premium: a full-service restaurant with a bar, or a bar attached to a destination the malls already feed. At 41 days this is the longest-standing licence on our New Jersey book, which tells you buyers are arguing with the price rather than with the town. Paramus hears transfers on the first and third Monday, and one Paramus transfer was refused in 2022, on a parking objection — worth reading before you pick a site with a small lot.

Process

What happens next, and how long each part takes

Day counts are medians from our own 168 closed files. They add up to 94 days, which is also the median time from accepted offer to a licence issued in the buyer's name.

  1. Offer accepted, contract signed7 days

    Deposit of 10% into escrow — $47,500 at this asking price. Contract is conditional on municipal approval; if the town refuses, the deposit comes back in full.

  2. Buyer qualification14 days

    Proof of funds and a completed ABC personal history. This is also the point the mutual NDA is signed and the full confidential file is released to you.

  3. Application filed21 days

    ABC-Form-12A to the municipality, with the transfer papers and the tax clearance request. Clearance itself typically takes 21 days and runs in parallel.

  4. Public notice published14 days

    Notice in the newspaper of record under N.J.A.C. 13:2-7.1. This is the window in which objections can be filed.

  5. Municipal hearing30 days

    Paramus's governing body hears transfers on the first and third Monday. The next filing deadline is 28 September 2026. Your counsel attends; you usually do too.

  6. Licence issued in your name8 days

    Resolution adopted, state paperwork completed, escrow released to the seller. You can trade.
