A Type 33 plenary retail consumption licence issued by the City of Vineland, in Cumberland County. Licence only — no premises, no fixtures, no goodwill. On-premise consumption of beer, wine and distilled spirits, with packaged sales confined to the principal public barroom (N.J.S.A. 33:1-12(1)).

Vineland is unusual for a South Jersey city: something near sixty-nine square miles of it, one of the largest municipal footprints in New Jersey. So a licence that transfers place-to-place anyplace inside the city line covers farmland, an industrial spine and a downtown all at once. On the governing body's approval you can move this licence a long way without leaving the municipality, and at this price that latitude is worth more than it sounds.

Process

What happens next, and how long each part takes

Day counts are medians from our own 168 closed files. They add up to 94 days, which is also the median time from accepted offer to a licence issued in the buyer's name.

  1. Offer accepted, contract signed7 days

    Deposit of 10% into escrow — $10,000 at this asking price. Contract is conditional on municipal approval; if the town refuses, the deposit comes back in full.

  2. Buyer qualification14 days

    Proof of funds and a completed ABC personal history. This is also the point the mutual NDA is signed and the full confidential file is released to you.

  3. Application filed21 days

    ABC-Form-12A to the municipality, with the transfer papers and the tax clearance request. Clearance itself typically takes 21 days and runs in parallel.

  4. Public notice published14 days

    Notice in the newspaper of record under N.J.A.C. 13:2-7.1. This is the window in which objections can be filed.

  5. Municipal hearing30 days

    Vineland's governing body hears transfers on the second and fourth Tuesday. The next filing deadline is 5 October 2026. Your counsel attends; you usually do too.

  6. Licence issued in your name8 days

    Resolution adopted, state paperwork completed, escrow released to the seller. You can trade.
