A Type 32 licence issued by the Borough of Somerville, in Somerset County — and the class is the whole point of it. A Type 32 is a consumption licence carrying a broader package privilege than a Type 33: everything a full bar may do, plus packaged goods may be sold from other than the principal public barroom (N.J.S.A. 33:1-12(1a)). Licence only: no premises, no fixtures, no goodwill.

In practice that means an off-sale counter alongside the bar rather than bottle sales squeezed into the barroom itself. Two revenue lines on one licence. It is why a Type 32 in a given town almost always asks more than a Type 33 there, and why this one asks the same $475,000 as the dearest Bergen Type 33 on our book while sitting in a county with a thinner consumption market. You are not paying for the county here. You are paying for the privilege.

Asked at $475,000, inside a $440,000–$500,000 band set on 25 August 2026. Somerset is not one of the counties for which the census publishes a median, so the honest comparison is statewide: $475,000 is above the measured $440,000 upper quartile of New Jersey asks and well above the $255,000 median. Our own Somerset file is 11 closed transfers with a $418,000 median on the Type 32 class — including a Somerville licence that settled at $392,000 in 2023.

Somerville's Main Street is a walkable restaurant row with a rail line into Newark and New York, which is exactly the trade a package-plus-consumption licence suits: a restaurant or wine bar with a retail wall along one side. Transfers are heard on the first and third Tuesday. Read the class wording before you compare this with a Type 33 — the broader privilege is the asset, and it is not priced into anything else on this book except the Union City licence.

Process

What happens next, and how long each part takes

Day counts are medians from our own 168 closed files. They add up to 94 days, which is also the median time from accepted offer to a licence issued in the buyer's name.

  1. Offer accepted, contract signed7 days

    Deposit of 10% into escrow — $47,500 at this asking price. Contract is conditional on municipal approval; if the town refuses, the deposit comes back in full.

  2. Buyer qualification14 days

    Proof of funds and a completed ABC personal history. This is also the point the mutual NDA is signed and the full confidential file is released to you.

  3. Application filed21 days

    ABC-Form-12A to the municipality, with the transfer papers and the tax clearance request. Clearance itself typically takes 21 days and runs in parallel.

  4. Public notice published14 days

    Notice in the newspaper of record under N.J.A.C. 13:2-7.1. This is the window in which objections can be filed.

  5. Municipal hearing30 days

    Somerville's governing body hears transfers on the first and third Tuesday. The next filing deadline is 30 September 2026. Your counsel attends; you usually do too.

  6. Licence issued in your name8 days

    Resolution adopted, state paperwork completed, escrow released to the seller. You can trade.
