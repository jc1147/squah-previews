The screening gap

Why a school screening isn't the whole picture

A school-nurse screening is quick by design. It checks how well a child reads letters on a distant chart, and little else. A child who can see the board but can't focus comfortably up close, whose eyes don't team together well, or who has an eye-health issue building quietly can pass that screening and still struggle. Families often find out only when grades slip or headaches start showing up in the middle of the semester.

We built this practice for Clear Lake and Bay Area families who want thorough, personal care rather than a rushed check. So here, we take the time to look at the whole picture, not just the chart on the wall.

  • Whether the eyes focus comfortably up close
  • How well the two eyes team together
  • Quiet, building eye-health problems

Inside the exam

Here it is a real comprehensive exam, not letters on a wall. Dr. Hyder checks visual acuity at both distance and near, how well the eyes focus and work together as a team, and takes a refraction to measure any glasses prescription. He also looks at your child's overall eye health.

It's the full pediatric exam, done carefully and unhurried, so nothing that matters for the classroom gets missed.

Common questions

Straight answers before you book

Isn't the school screening enough?

A screening is a fast distance check, and it's a helpful early flag, but it isn't a full exam. It can miss focusing problems, eye-teaming issues, and eye-health concerns. A comprehensive exam looks at all of it.

What age should my child start?

We see children from about age 4 to 5. If you have a question about a younger child, call us at (281) 488-0066 and we'll help you decide when the time is right.

Is it covered by insurance?

We accept most vision and medical plans, including VSP, EyeMed, Superior, Aetna, BCBS, Medicare, Cigna, and Tricare. Coverage varies by plan, so call (281) 488-0066 and we'll help you check yours before the visit.

My child struggles with reading. Do you offer therapy programs for that?

We focus on comprehensive eye exams, and we don't offer therapy programs or eye exercises for reading difficulties here. If your child's exam points to a need for that kind of care, we'll help point you toward the right specialist.
