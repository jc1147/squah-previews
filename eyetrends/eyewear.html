Why buy your eyewear here

A frame fitted to your face, not guessed at from a screen

At a value chain's optical counter, or on an eyewear website, buying glasses tends to go one of two ways: a rushed fifteen minutes and a coupon-driven upsell, or a pair guessed at entirely from a photo - the wrong bridge for your nose, a measurement that is a few millimeters off, a prescription nobody really sat down and checked. We are not an America's Best, and we do not run the shop like one.

Here you browse first, on your own time, and then a doctor who takes the time fits the frame to your face and confirms your current Rx before a single lens is ordered. We talk, we visit, and we get the fit right - instead of moving you through in fifteen minutes and sending you out the door.

The doctor behind the fitting

The same Clear Lake doctor since 1984

You will wear these on your face every day, so it is fair to ask who is behind the fitting. Dr. Jerry Hyder has cared for Clear Lake and the Bay Area since 1984 - 42 years fitting eyewear for local families.

Some have been with him for 40 years, and now bring their own kids in for a first pair. That continuity is the whole reason to choose a person over a warehouse: one doctor, who knows your eyes, standing across the counter when you pick your glasses up.
