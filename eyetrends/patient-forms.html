New patient forms - Clear Lake

New here? A few quiet minutes now, and your first visit starts where it should.

Welcome. If this is your first visit, you can fill out your new-patient history and intake before you come in, from your own kitchen table, at your own pace. It is a small thing that makes a real difference. Here, an eye exam is unhurried on purpose: we take about thirty minutes, we talk, we visit, and we get to know you. Doing your paperwork ahead of time is what protects those minutes, so your appointment can start with your eyes and your questions, not with a stack of paper on a clipboard.

Before you arrive

Two things to complete, a few things to bring

Before your visit, there are just two things to fill out. The first is a short new-patient registration. The second is a medical and vision history. Take your time with it. Nothing needs to be printed or notarized ahead of time, and if you would rather fill the forms in with us at the front desk, that is perfectly fine too.

New-patient registration

A short new-patient registration you can complete before you arrive.

Medical and vision history

Any conditions or surgeries, the medications and allergies you have, your family's eye history, and the reason for your visit.

Bring on the day

On the day of your appointment, bring a few things with you:

  • A photo ID
  • Your vision or medical insurance card
  • Your current glasses, or a copy of your prescription
  • A list of the medications you take

We are an insurance-based practice. We work with both vision and medical plans, and we also see patients on a self-pay basis, with CareCredit available if that helps. Bringing your insurance card lets the front desk verify your plan before you sit down with the doctor, so there are no hold-ups once your visit begins.

How billing works

What will this cost? Call us and we will give you a real number.

This is usually the next question, and it is a fair one. We do not post a price list, because what you will pay depends on your insurance and on what your visit actually involves. We would rather give you a real number than a wrong one. Call us at (281) 488-0066 before your appointment and we will walk you through it in plain terms. Alongside insurance, we accept CareCredit and cash.

No published prices

No surprise fees, no pressure, just a clear answer when you ask.

A little thing, done for decades

A little thing, and part of how we have always worked

Filling out your forms ahead is a little thing, but it is part of how this practice has cared for Clear Lake families since 1984. We would rather spend your visit getting to know you and your eyes than shuffling paperwork. The same doctor, Dr. Jerry Hyder, has worked this way for 42 years, and it is the reason a first appointment here tends to feel like a conversation rather than a conveyor belt.

You are a person to us before you are a chart, and the few minutes you save us at the desk are minutes we give back to you in the exam room.
