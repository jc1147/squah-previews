Why an exam here is different

The same doctor, every visit - not whoever is on shift

At a big-box counter or a rotating multi-doctor group, you get whoever is on the schedule that day: a quick check, a coupon, and a face you may not see again next year. When six doctors share one roster, your history gets handed off, and you end up re-explaining yourself at every appointment.

Here it works the other way. You see the same doctor each time, which means we can slow down, compare this year's eyes to last year's, and talk through what has actually changed - the headaches at your screen, the glare when you drive at night, the small print that is not as easy as it used to be. An eye exam should feel like a visit with someone who knows you, not a conveyor belt.
