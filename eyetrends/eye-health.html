Kids' vision · Featured

Featured guide · 5 min read

Kids' vision in Clear Lake - what parents should watch for, and when a squint or a headache means it is time for an exam.

If you have ever watched your child sit too close to the screen, rub their eyes after homework, or come home with headaches, you already know the worry. Most of the time it is nothing serious. Sometimes it is a sign their eyes need a little help. This guide walks through the everyday things parents notice - squinting, tilting the head, losing their place while reading, eyes that drift or cross - and tells you plainly which ones can wait for a routine check and which ones are worth a call.

We see children here from about age 4 or 5, and families come back year after year because the same doctor knows their kids. If something does need attention, we can usually find a time that works - we hold Saturday morning hours, and we keep room for same-day visits when a child's eye is red, hurting, or clearly not right. You do not have to figure out on your own whether it can wait.
